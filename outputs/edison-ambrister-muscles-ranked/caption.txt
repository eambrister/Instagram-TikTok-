Not every muscle grows at the same speed.

For most people, it goes something like this:

Easiest: glutes, traps, quads.
Steady with work: chest, biceps, triceps, lats.
Need focused volume: side delts, rear delts, hamstrings.
Most stubborn: calves, forearms.

Your genetics will shuffle this list. Some people grow calves just walking around. Some fight for every millimetre.

And abs? You build them with training, but you only see them when your body fat is low enough.

Stubborn doesn't mean impossible. It means more weekly sets, better consistency and more patience.
