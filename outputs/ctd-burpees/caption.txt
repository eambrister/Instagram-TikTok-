100 BURPEES CHANGED MY CONDITIONING.

But I didn't start at 100.

I started with what I could do. Ten became 20. Twenty became 50. Eventually, I worked my way up to 100 without stopping.

Burpees combine floor-to-standing transitions, bodyweight support and, depending on the variation, a jump or push-up. Together, those demands challenge cardiovascular fitness, muscular endurance and coordination.

No fancy equipment. Just a movement you can progressively get better at.

The goal is to build capacity with repetitions you can control. Keep a steady pace. Land softly when you jump. Slow down or rest when your technique starts to slip.

YOUR CHALLENGE: Try 10 controlled burpees today. Use a step-back variation, split them into smaller sets, or start with fewer.

You don't need to start at 100. Start where you are, recover between sessions and let consistency do the work.

Discipline - Accountability - Consistency.
