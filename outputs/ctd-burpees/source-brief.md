# CUTTHROAT DISCIPLINE

## Instagram Carousel: WHY BURPEES ARE ONE OF THE GREATEST CONDITIONING EXERCISES EVER

The concept is a premium, cinematic, 3D sports-performance campaign that combines high-end athletic photography, realistic anatomical visualizations, dramatic lighting, and editorial typography.

Think Nike campaign meets elite sports science documentary, with the aggressive identity of Cutthroat Discipline.

The carousel will have 8 slides, each with its own visual concept, headline, supporting copy, and highly detailed AI image-generation prompt.

## 1. The visual identity

Obsidian black · Titanium silver · Deep crimson · Warm white

Design specifications

- Format: Instagram portrait carousel, 1080 × 1350 pixels (4:5).
- Heading font: Heading Now 31–38, heavy condensed uppercase.
- Body font: Gotham, medium or book.
- Visual style: Hyperrealistic 3D, premium sports advertising, physically accurate anatomy, volumetric lighting, and editorial composition.
- Subject: The same lean, muscular, athletic male throughout the carousel, with realistic proportions and no exaggerated muscle size.
- Backgrounds: Distinct environments that directly illustrate each slide's message, rather than repeating the same dark gym.
- Branding: Small CUTTHROAT DISCIPLINE wordmark, consistent slide numbering, and understated graphic accents.

The image prompts below are designed to produce the visual backgrounds. For the cleanest result, generate the images without text, then add the exact typography in Canva.

## 4. Important creative recommendations

The biggest mistake would be making every slide feature a muscular man doing burpees against a dark background. That would become visually repetitive by slide three.

This concept instead uses eight distinct visual stories: explosive movement, anatomical muscle activation, cardiovascular physiology, power, equipment-free training, fatigue, progression, and personal achievement.

For credibility, avoid claiming that burpees are scientifically the single best conditioning exercise or that they automatically build maximum strength, burn a fixed number of calories, or produce superior fat loss. Their strength is the combination of multiple physical demands in one accessible movement.

One final recommendation: Use your actual physique as the reference for the athlete in the carousel. This will make the content recognizable as Cutthroat Discipline rather than generic fitness content, without artificially enhancing your body.
