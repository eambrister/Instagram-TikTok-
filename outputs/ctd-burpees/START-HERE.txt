CUT THROAT DISCIPLINE — BURPEES

1. Save the eight numbered PNG images to your device.
2. Create an Instagram carousel and select slides 01 through 08 in order.
3. Use the portrait 4:5 crop. Every slide is 1080 x 1350 pixels.
4. Copy caption.txt into the post. Optional image descriptions are in alt-text.txt.
5. Review each slide before posting.

preview.jpg is an overview for review; post the eight separate PNGs.
The PNGs can also be used for a TikTok photo post; check the app preview before publishing.

Editable SVGs, scene assets and font files are available in the GitHub folder:
https://github.com/eambrister/Instagram-TikTok-/tree/main/outputs/ctd-burpees

Typography uses the accepted temporary substitutes Barlow Condensed ExtraBold
and Montserrat Medium/Bold. The confirmed personal story is in the caption.
