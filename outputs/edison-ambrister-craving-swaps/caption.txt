Cravings aren't the enemy.
Having no plan for them is.

A craving doesn't mean you failed, and it isn't a sign your body is missing something. Usually you're hungry, tired, stressed or bored. The fix is having something better ready before the craving hits.

Craving sugar?
Swap it for protein and fruit: Greek yogurt with berries, eggs, apple with peanut butter. Sweet enough, and it keeps you full.

Craving chocolate?
Have it, but portion it. Two squares of dark chocolate, almonds, strawberries or cocoa yogurt.

Craving salty snacks?
Get the crunch without the fryer: air-popped popcorn, salted edamame, cucumber with sea salt, olives.

Craving carbs?
Carbs aren't the problem. Oversized portions and the extras are. Rice, potatoes, oats and pasta, measured.

The rule: don't ban it, plan it. Eat your protein first, then decide if you still want it.
