What to eat around training, kept simple.

Before (1 to 2 hours out): easy carbs and a little protein. Banana, oats, Greek yogurt, a bit of honey.

During: for most sessions under an hour, water is enough. Training longer? Dates or a banana for quick carbs.

After (within a few hours): protein plus carbs. Salmon, eggs, white rice, sweet potato.

The truth is that your total protein and calories for the day matter far more than exact timing. Get the daily basics right first, and this is the bonus.
