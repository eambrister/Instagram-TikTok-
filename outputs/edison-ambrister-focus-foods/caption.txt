Foods for focus, without the hype.

Eggs: protein and choline.
Chicken: protein, including tyrosine.
Pumpkin seeds: iron and magnesium.
Oats: slow, steady carbs.
Almonds: healthy fats and vitamin E.
Dark chocolate: a little caffeine. Keep it to a small portion.

But let's be honest: no food is a dopamine hack. Eating tyrosine doesn't flood your brain with dopamine.

What actually sharpens focus is sleep, morning daylight, training and fewer screens. Food supports it. It doesn't replace it.
