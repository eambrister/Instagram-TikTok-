SAME STARTING WEIGHT. DIFFERENT STANDARDS.

One person trains when motivation appears.
The other follows the schedule.

One guesses every meal and keeps starting over.
The other tracks, reviews and adjusts.

Both people can want the same result. Their repeated decisions will produce completely different outcomes.

I started at 222 pounds. Losing 80 pounds and keeping it off took almost two years. It did not come from one perfect workout or one perfect meal. It came from executing the right decisions long enough for the result to become undeniable.

Your goals do not record what you intended to do. They record what you repeatedly execute.

Which side are you living on right now?
