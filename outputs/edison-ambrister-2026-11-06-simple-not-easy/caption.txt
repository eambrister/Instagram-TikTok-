Losing weight is simple.
It isn't easy.

The basics fit on one slide:
1. Eat fewer calories than you burn.
2. Hit your protein.
3. Train 3+ days a week.
4. Repeat it for months.

Everyone knows that. So why do most people still struggle? Not because they need a better plan. Because of these:

1. You're guessing your portions.
It's easy to underestimate what you eat. Weigh and log your food for one week and you'll see where the calories really come from.

2. Your kitchen works against you.
If it's in the house, it gets eaten. Keep protein ready to go and keep the foods you can't moderate out of reach.

3. You're waiting to feel ready.
Motivation usually shows up after you start, not before. Begin with the smallest version and let momentum build.

4. You quit when it gets boring.
Results come from repeating ordinary days for months. Boring doesn't mean it isn't working.

The plan isn't the problem. Repeating it is.
