Your body is built from your plate.

Every rep you do needs raw material. Here's what 6 parts of your body run on, built from whole foods you can buy this week:

Muscles: chicken breast, eggs, lean beef, Greek yogurt. Protein gives your body the building blocks to repair and grow after training.

Heart: salmon, oats, walnuts, lentils and beans. Staples of heart-healthy eating patterns.

Bones: sardines (bones in), Greek yogurt, tofu, kale. Calcium-rich foods help keep bones strong.

Gut: oats, beans, banana, yogurt with live cultures. Fibre and fermented foods feed your gut.

Brain: salmon, walnuts, eggs, blueberries. Omega-3 fats, choline and colour.

Eyes: carrots, sweet potato, spinach, egg yolk. Vitamin A and lutein.

No single food fixes anything. What changes your body is putting these on your plate week after week.

Save this for your next grocery run, and follow for more ways to feed the body you're training.
