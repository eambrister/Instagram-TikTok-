## 01 · Testosterone nutrients: 6 foods

- **Title:** Testosterone Nutrients: 6 Foods High in Zinc, Vitamin D & Selenium
- **Description:** Zinc, vitamin D and selenium help keep hormone levels normal, and food is the simplest place to get them. Oysters and lean beef for zinc, salmon and eggs for vitamin D, and one or two Brazil nuts for selenium (no more than that). Food covers a shortfall. It won't raise you above normal. If your energy or libido is low, ask a doctor for bloodwork. Save this for your next grocery run. #mensnutrition #testosterone #zincfoods #healthyeating
- **Alt text:** List of six foods on a black background: oysters, lean beef and pumpkin seeds for zinc, salmon and eggs for vitamin D, and Brazil nuts for selenium, each with an approximate amount.

## 02 · 6 food swaps that helped me lose 80 lb

- **Title:** 6 Food Swaps That Helped Me Lose 80 Pounds (Fat Loss Meal Ideas)
- **Description:** I went from 222 to 142 pounds in under two years. These six swaps did a lot of that work: pancakes became oatmeal or cream of wheat with fruit, pizza night became a lean beef and rice bowl, and takeout became meals I could prep and repeat. No food is good or bad. The swaps just meant fewer calories, more protein and more food I could keep eating. That's my experience, not a promise of your result. #fatlossmeals #weightlossjourney #healthyswaps #highprotein
- **Alt text:** Before-and-now table on cream: pancakes become oatmeal with fruit, pizza becomes a lean beef rice bowl, ribs and mac become chicken, potatoes and vegetables, sushi becomes homemade tuna and rice, cake becomes fruit, random eating becomes counted repeatable meals.

## 03 · The fat-loss fridge setup

- **Title:** Fat Loss Fridge Setup: How to Organize Your Fridge for Meal Prep
- **Description:** Make the plan the easiest thing to grab. Fruit goes where you can see it, cooked protein goes at eye level, and ready carbs go on the middle shelf. Cut your vegetables on Sunday and keep a freezer backup for bad weeks. If it's prepped and visible, it gets eaten. Save this before your next Sunday prep. #mealprep #fridgeorganization #fatloss #healthyhabits
- **Alt text:** Simple fridge diagram beside six numbered zones: fruit at the top front, portioned protein at eye level, cooked carbs on the middle shelf, prepped vegetables in the drawers, measured sauces in the door, and frozen backups in the freezer.

## 05 · 60 g protein shake

- **Title:** 60g High Protein Shake Recipe (Breakfast Meal Replacement)
- **Description:** A full breakfast in one glass with about 61 g of protein and 670 kcal. Blend 1 cup skim milk, 1 scoop whey, 170 g plain nonfat Greek yogurt, 40 g rolled oats, a banana, 1 tbsp peanut butter and 1 tbsp cocoa with ice. Cutting? Drop the oats and peanut butter for about 52 g protein and 420 kcal. Values are approximate, so check your labels. #proteinshake #highproteinrecipes #musclebuilding #mealprep
- **Alt text:** Recipe card on cream listing seven shake ingredients with amounts, protein and calories, with totals of about 61 g protein, 670 kcal, 81 g carbs and 14 g fat.

## Sources and assumptions

- Zinc, vitamin D and selenium amounts come from the NIH Office of Dietary Supplements fact sheets. Brazil nuts carry about 68–91 mcg of selenium each, and the adult upper limit is 400 mcg per day, which is why the pin says 1–2 nuts.
- Shake macros use typical USDA label values. Whey and yogurt vary by brand.
- The 222 → 142 lb figure is Edison's confirmed personal result and is labeled as his experience.
