Lean or bulk. Pick one and commit.

Staying lean gets you the six pack and the definition, but you can feel small in a T-shirt.

Bulking gets you size and strength, but the abs go into hiding for a while.

Neither one is wrong. What's wrong is switching every three weeks and ending up with neither.

Choose the goal that fits this season, give it a few months, then reassess.
