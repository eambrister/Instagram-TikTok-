Your workout only counts if you recover from it.

Short on sleep, most people notice the same things: slower recovery, more hunger and cravings, foggy focus and a shorter fuse. That makes the whole plan harder to stick to.

With 7 to 9 hours, recovery is easier, hunger is steadier and you show up sharper for training.

Adults need at least 7 hours a night. Treat it like part of the program:
Same bedtime most nights.
Screens down 30 minutes before bed.
Caffeine done by early afternoon.
Dark, cool room.

How many hours did you get last night? Be honest in the comments.
