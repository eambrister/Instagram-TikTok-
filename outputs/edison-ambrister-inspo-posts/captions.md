## 05 · 60 g protein shake

**TikTok title:** 60g protein shake for busy mornings

```
No time for breakfast? This is 60 grams of protein in one glass.

Blend with ice for 45 seconds:
• 1 cup skim milk
• 1 scoop whey (30 g)
• 170 g plain nonfat Greek yogurt
• 40 g rolled oats
• 1 medium banana
• 1 tbsp peanut butter
• 1 tbsp unsweetened cocoa

That's about 61 g protein, 670 kcal, 81 g carbs and 14 g fat.

Cutting? Drop the oats and peanut butter for about 52 g protein and 420 kcal.

Values are approximate and brands vary, so check your labels.

Save it and make it tomorrow morning.

## 02 · 6 food swaps that helped me lose 80 lb

**TikTok title:** 6 food swaps that helped me lose 80 lb

```
I didn't stop eating. I changed what was on the plate.

Pancakes with syrup → oatmeal or cream of wheat with fruit
Pizza night → lean ground beef and rice bowl
Ribs, mac and coleslaw → chicken breast, potatoes and vegetables
Sushi and spring rolls → tuna and rice, made at home
Cake and candy → fruit
Eating whatever was there → repeatable meals, counted

None of those old foods are "bad." For me, they were easy to overeat and hard to stop. The swaps meant fewer calories, more protein, and meals I could repeat every week.

I went from 222 to 142 lb in under two years. That's my experience, not a promise of your result.

Which swap would be hardest for you?
