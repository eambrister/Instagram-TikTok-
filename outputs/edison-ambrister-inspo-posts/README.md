# Inspo posts (6 single-image posts)

Six original 1080 × 1350 (4:5) posts for Instagram and TikTok photo mode, in the core Edison Ambrister kit. They were inspired by Pinterest reference graphics, but every concept was rewritten around Edison's own foods, story and standards. They contain no photos, AI imagery or body-comparison visuals.

- [Download all posts (ZIP)](https://github.com/eambrister/Instagram-TikTok-/raw/main/outputs/edison-ambrister-inspo-posts/inspo-posts.zip)
- [Preview all six](overview.jpg)
- [Captions, TikTok titles and schedule](captions.md)

Use the PNGs for Instagram and the JPEGs for TikTok, which rejects PNG photos.

| File | Series | Concept |
|---|---|---|
| `01-testosterone-nutrient-foods` | EDISON EATS | Zinc, vitamin D and selenium foods, with honest "normal levels" framing |
| `02-food-swaps-80-lb` | MY PROOF | Edison's own before-and-now food swaps |
| `03-fat-loss-fridge-setup` | EXECUTION SYSTEMS | Fridge zones for meal prep |
| `04-choose-your-hard` | THE STANDARD | Typography statement |
| `05-60g-protein-shake` | EDISON EATS | Shake recipe with per-ingredient macros |
| `06-fat-loss-grocery-list` | MY PROOF | Edison's 10 weekly staples |

Rebuild with `node tools/build_edison_ambrister_inspo_posts.js`.
