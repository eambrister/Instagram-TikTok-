THE HOLIDAY UNHOLY TRINITY IS HERE.

October brings the candy and parties.

November makes overeating feel like an obligation.

December adds alcohol, travel, desserts, poor sleep, and even more skipped workouts.

One holiday meal will not destroy your progress. The real danger is allowing one celebration to become three months without standards.

If you have been consistent all year, this season can break the routine and self-trust you worked hard to build.

If you have not started yet, waiting until January gives the problem another three months to grow.

You do not need to avoid every party or reject every holiday meal. You need boundaries:

- Keep a minimum training schedule.
- Prioritize protein and whole foods most of the time.
- Maintain your daily movement.
- Choose the events and foods that matter to you.
- Decide your alcohol limit before you start drinking.
- Return to your routine at the next opportunity.

Enjoy the holidays. Just do not turn a celebration into a 90-day surrender.
