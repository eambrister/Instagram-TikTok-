Here is a detailed creative prompt you can use to produce the carousel. I strengthened the concept by making one distinction clear: **the holidays themselves do not wreck progress; turning every celebration into permission to abandon your standards does.** That keeps the message hard-hitting without sounding like food, family gatherings, or one indulgent meal should be feared.

# CUTTHROAT DISCIPLINE Carousel Prompt
## The Holiday Unholy Trinity

Create a nine-slide cinematic Instagram carousel for **CUTTHROAT DISCIPLINE**, a premium fitness, accountability, and discipline brand.

The campaign is titled:

# THE HOLIDAY
# UNHOLY TRINITY

The “Unholy Trinity” represents:

- October: Halloween
- November: Thanksgiving
- December: Christmas

The carousel should warn viewers that these three months can become one long period of overeating, drinking, partying, poor sleep, reduced movement, and skipped workouts.

The central message is:

> **One holiday meal will not destroy your progress. Three months of repeatedly abandoning your standards can.**

People who trained consistently all year risk losing their routine and momentum. People who have not started exercising risk entering January even further away from their goals.

The carousel should feel confrontational, cinematic, premium, and slightly ominous while remaining educational and practical.

## Format

- Nine individual slides
- Exact dimensions: 1080 × 1350 pixels
- Exact aspect ratio: 4:5
- Designed for Instagram and TikTok photo carousels
- Keep all essential text inside 65–75 pixel safe margins
- Make every slide readable on a phone
- Do not create a square carousel
- Do not place all slides on one landscape canvas

## Brand identity

Use the official gold-and-silver circular **CUTTHROAT DISCIPLINE** logo on every slide.

Do not redraw, reinterpret, or replace the logo.

Use this exact footer:

**DISCIPLINE - ACCOUNTABILITY - CONSISTENCY**

Position the logo consistently near the upper-left corner. Place the slide number near the upper-right:

**01 / 09**

## Typography

Headlines should resemble **Heading Now 31–38 Bold**.

If unavailable, use **Barlow Condensed ExtraBold**.

Supporting copy should use **Gotham**.

If unavailable, use **Montserrat Medium or Bold**.

Typography must be:

- Bold and condensed
- Naturally proportioned
- Large enough for mobile reading
- Carefully spaced
- Hierarchical rather than crowded
- Integrated with the imagery without covering important objects

Never stretch or compress lettering to fill the canvas.

## Color palette

Use the established CUTTHROAT DISCIPLINE palette:

- Obsidian black: `#090A0C`
- Charcoal: `#15171B`
- Titanium silver: `#AEB3BA`
- Warm off-white: `#F3EFE6`
- Metallic gold: `#D6A928`
- Gold highlight: `#F1CF63`
- Deep crimson: `#8D1721`

Gold should emphasize the most important words.

Use crimson sparingly to communicate danger, warning, or decline.

## Image direction

Create hyperrealistic, cinematic holiday still-life imagery with premium commercial lighting.

The campaign should feel like a luxury sports advertisement combined with a dark seasonal editorial.

Use holiday objects and environments rather than artificial-looking people. If people appear, show only natural, anonymous details such as a hand reaching for a drink or an out-of-focus party crowd. Do not generate prominent faces or unrealistic physiques.

Every image must leave intentional negative space for typography.

Do not generate text, letters, logos, labels, or watermarks inside the images. Add all typography and branding during layout.

Avoid:

- Cartoon Halloween imagery
- Comedic costumes
- Cheap Christmas graphics
- Excessive neon
- Generic stock-photo compositions
- Grotesque food imagery
- Body shaming
- Before-and-after bodies
- Fearmongering about one meal
- Claims that three holidays automatically erase a year of progress

# Accuracy and messaging guardrails

- Do not claim that one meal causes meaningful fat gain.
- Do not claim that three holidays automatically erase an entire year.
- Do not present specific calorie or weight-gain statistics without a cited source.
- Do not shame cultural foods or family celebrations.
- Do not tell viewers they must “earn” food through exercise.
- Focus on repeated behavior, routine disruption, alcohol consumption, sleep, movement, and the all-or-nothing mindset.
- Frame the solution as boundaries and rapid return, rather than punishment or extreme restriction.
- Keep the campaign hard-hitting while giving viewers a realistic system they can follow.
