October. November. December.
Three celebrations can become one long excuse to abandon the standards you built all year.

02 — THE 90-DAY SLIDE
One meal isn't the problem. The 90-day slide is.
Repeated exceptions become a new routine.

03 — OCTOBER
Halloween opens the door.
Candy stays visible. Parties multiply. One missed workout becomes “I'll restart Monday.”

04 — NOVEMBER
Excess starts to feel mandatory.
The meal isn't the problem. The celebration becomes a weekend; the weekend becomes another postponed week.

05 — DECEMBER
December finishes what October started. Then January gets blamed.

06 — TWO PATHS
If you've been locked in, protect what you built.
If you haven't started, stop delaying the start.

07 — HOW PROGRESS ERODES
More liquid calories. More frequent overeating. Fewer workouts. Less daily movement. Poorer sleep and recovery.

08 — THE HOLIDAY DEFENSE SYSTEM
Enjoy the holiday. Keep your standards. You don't need perfection; you need boundaries.

09 — THE CLOSING STANDARD
January should not have to rescue you.
Enjoy the season. Don't abandon yourself.
