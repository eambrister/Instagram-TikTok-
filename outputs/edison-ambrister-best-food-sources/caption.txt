Most people don't get enough of these four nutrients, and supplements aren't the only fix. Here are the best whole-food sources of each. They're not ranked, just the best.

Vitamin B12: clams, beef liver, salmon, sardines, beef, eggs. B12 is found naturally only in animal foods, so if you eat plant-based, use fortified foods or a supplement.

Heme iron: beef liver, oysters, beef, sardines, mussels, lamb. Your body absorbs heme iron better than iron from plants. If you're eating plant iron, pair it with vitamin C.

Zinc: oysters, beef, crab, lamb, pumpkin seeds, cheese. Oysters carry more zinc per serving than any other food.

Omega-3 (EPA and DHA): salmon, mackerel, sardines, herring, anchovies, trout. Aim for two servings of oily fish a week.

You don't need all of them. Pick two from each list and get them into your week.
