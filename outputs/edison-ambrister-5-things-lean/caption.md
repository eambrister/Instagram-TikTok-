If you're not getting lean, one of these 5 is missing.

Every single day:
1. Sleep. 7–8 hours. That's where recovery happens.
2. Calories. Know your number. Deficit to lose, surplus to build. Hit it either way.
3. Protein. I don't chase exact carbs and fats. I make sure protein is hit.
4. Steps. 10k, 15k, 20k, whatever your number is. Done before bed.
5. Training. 3, 4 or 5 days a week. Pick your style and actually show up.

None of this is complicated. It's just daily.
This is how I went from 222 to about 142 and kept it off. Your timeline will look different. The standard doesn't.

Save this. Then comment the one you miss most.
