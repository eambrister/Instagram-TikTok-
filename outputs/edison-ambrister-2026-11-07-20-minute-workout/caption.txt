No gym this weekend?
You still have 20 minutes and the floor.

Here's a full-body workout with no equipment:

Format: 4 rounds. 40 seconds of work, 20 seconds of rest. 30 seconds of rest between rounds. About 20 minutes total.

Warm up first: 2–3 minutes of marching, arm circles and easy squats.

1. Bodyweight squats
Sit back and down as far as you can control, then drive up through your heels. Easier: squat to a chair.

2. Push-ups
Body in one straight line, chest toward the floor. Easier: hands on a bench or knees down.

3. Reverse lunges
Step back until both knees bend to about 90 degrees, then alternate legs. Easier: hold a wall.

4. Plank
Squeeze your glutes and abs and keep your hips level. Easier: knees down.

5. Jumping jacks
A steady pace you can hold for 40 seconds. Easier: step jacks.

Go easier, not harder, the first time. Stop if anything hurts. The goal today is to finish, not to collapse.

A short workout you do beats a perfect one you skip.

Comment "DONE" when you finish all 4 rounds.
