20-MINUTE NO-EQUIPMENT WORKOUT

01 EDISON TRAINS | NO GYM / THIS WEEKEND? | NO PROBLEM. | A 20-minute, no-equipment workout you can do at home. Save it, then do it.
02 HOW IT WORKS | 4 ROUNDS. / ABOUT 20 MINUTES. | 40 SECONDS WORK / 20 SECONDS REST / 5 EXERCISES PER ROUND / 30 SECONDS REST BETWEEN ROUNDS | Warm up first: 2–3 minutes of marching, arm circles and easy squats.
03 EXERCISE 01 | BODYWEIGHT / SQUATS. | Feet shoulder-width, chest up. Sit back and down as far as you can control, then drive up through your heels. Easier: squat to a chair.
04 EXERCISE 02 | PUSH-UPS. | Hands under shoulders, body in one straight line. Lower your chest toward the floor and press back up. Easier: hands on a bench or knees down.
05 EXERCISE 03 | REVERSE / LUNGES. | Step back and lower until both knees bend to about 90 degrees. Push back up and alternate legs. Easier: hold a wall for balance.
06 EXERCISE 04 | PLANK. | Forearms under shoulders. Squeeze your glutes and abs and keep your hips level with your shoulders. Easier: knees down.
07 EXERCISE 05 | JUMPING / JACKS. | Keep a steady pace you can hold for the full 40 seconds. Easier: step jacks, no jumping.
08 YOUR TURN | COMMENT | “DONE.” | When you finish all 4 rounds. Stop if anything hurts. Go easier, not harder, the first time.
