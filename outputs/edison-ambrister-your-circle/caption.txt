Who you surround yourself with matters.

One rotten orange doesn't stay alone for long. The rot spreads to whatever is next to it.

Same with people. Spend your time around excuses, laziness and negativity, and it seeps into you. Spend it around people who train, build and keep their word, and that spreads too.

You don't have to cut everyone off. But be honest about who's pulling you forward and who's pulling you down.

Tag someone who makes you better.
