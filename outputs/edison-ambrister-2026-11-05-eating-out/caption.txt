Eating out isn't the problem.
Eating out with no plan is.

You don't need to skip dinner with friends to lose fat. You need a few rules you bring to the table.

1. Decide before you arrive.
Look at the menu ahead of time and pick your order while you're not hungry.

2. Build around protein.
Grilled chicken, fish, steak or shrimp first. Then add vegetables and one carb.

3. Watch the extras.
Sauces, dressings, butter and sugary drinks can add hundreds of calories without filling you up. Get sauce on the side and drink water.

4. Pick one indulgence.
Dessert, a drink or the bread basket. Choose the one you actually want and skip the rest.

5. Stop at satisfied.
Restaurant portions are often bigger than you need. Eat slowly, stop when you're satisfied and take the rest home.

One meal out never ruined anyone's progress. Writing off the rest of the week does.

Save this before your next night out, so you have it at the table.
