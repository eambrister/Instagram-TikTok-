HOW TO EAT OUT WITHOUT LOSING THE WEEK

01 EDISON EATS | EATING OUT / ISN'T THE / PROBLEM. | NO PLAN IS. | 5 rules for restaurants, takeaway and nights out, without skipping the fun.
02 RULE 01 | DECIDE BEFORE / YOU ARRIVE. | Look at the menu ahead of time and pick your order while you're not hungry.
03 RULE 02 | BUILD AROUND / PROTEIN. | Grilled chicken, fish, steak or shrimp first. Then add vegetables and one carb.
04 RULE 03 | WATCH THE / EXTRAS. | Sauces, dressings, butter and sugary drinks can add hundreds of calories without filling you up. Get sauce on the side and drink water.
05 RULE 04 | PICK ONE / INDULGENCE. | Dessert, a drink or the bread basket. Choose the one you actually want and skip the rest.
06 RULE 05 | STOP AT / SATISFIED. | Restaurant portions are often bigger than you need. Eat slowly, stop when you're satisfied and take the rest home.
07 ONE MEAL OUT NEVER | RUINED / ANYONE'S / PROGRESS. | Writing off the rest of the week does.
08 SAVE THIS | BEFORE YOUR / NEXT | NIGHT OUT. | So you have it at the table.
