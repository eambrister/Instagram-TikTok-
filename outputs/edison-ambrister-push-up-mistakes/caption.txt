Your push-ups might be working against you.

Three mistakes I see all the time:

1. Hips sagging. Your lower back takes the load instead of your chest and core.
2. Elbows flared out to 90°. Hard on the shoulders and weaker at the bottom.
3. Head cranked up. Keep your neck neutral and your eyes just ahead of your hands.

The fix: squeeze your glutes, brace your abs, keep one straight line from head to heels, and tuck your elbows to about 45°. Lower with control.

Ten clean reps beat thirty sloppy ones. Form over reps, every time.
