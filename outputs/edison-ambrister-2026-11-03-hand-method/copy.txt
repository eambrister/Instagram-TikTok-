THE HAND METHOD: PORTIONS WITHOUT A SCALE

01 EDISON EATS | NO FOOD / SCALE? | USE YOUR / HAND. | A simple way to portion a fat-loss plate anywhere: at home, at work or eating out.
02 THE PLATE | YOUR HAND IS / YOUR SCALE. | PALM = PROTEIN / FIST = VEGETABLES / CUPPED HAND = CARBS / THUMB = FATS | It goes everywhere with you, and it scales to your size.
03 PROTEIN | 1–2 PALMS / OF PROTEIN. | Chicken, fish, lean beef, eggs or tuna. A palm is the size and thickness of your palm. Start every plate here.
04 VEGETABLES | 1–2 FISTS / OF VEGETABLES. | Volume that fills you up for very few calories. Fresh or frozen, any vegetable counts.
05 CARBS | 1–2 CUPPED / HANDS OF CARBS. | Rice, potatoes, oats or fruit. Go up on hard training days. Go down if your progress stalls for 2–3 weeks.
06 FATS | 1–2 THUMBS / OF FATS. | Oil, butter, nuts, cheese and sauces. Small amounts carry a lot of calories, so this is the one to watch.
07 THE BEST PORTION SYSTEM | IS THE ONE / YOU'LL USE. | Use the lower end of each range to start. Adjust based on your weekly trend.
08 SAVE THIS | FOR YOUR / NEXT | MEAL. | Build your next plate with it. Then build the same plate again tomorrow.
