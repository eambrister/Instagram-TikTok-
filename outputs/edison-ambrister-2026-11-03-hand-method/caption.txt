No food scale?
Use your hand.

Weighing everything works, but most people won't do it forever. The hand method gives you portions you can repeat anywhere: at home, at work or eating out. Your hand goes everywhere with you, and it scales to your size.

Per meal:

Protein: 1–2 palms.
Chicken, fish, lean beef, eggs or tuna. Start every plate here.

Vegetables: 1–2 fists.
Volume that fills you up for very few calories. Fresh or frozen, any vegetable counts.

Carbs: 1–2 cupped hands.
Rice, potatoes, oats or fruit. Go up on hard training days. Go down if your progress stalls for 2–3 weeks.

Fats: 1–2 thumbs.
Oil, butter, nuts, cheese and sauces. Small amounts carry a lot of calories, so this is the one to watch.

Start at the lower end of each range and adjust based on your weekly trend, not one day on the scale.

The best portion system is the one you'll actually use. Perfect tracking you quit doesn't help. Simple portions you repeat do.
