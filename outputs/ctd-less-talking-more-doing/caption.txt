Talking about the goal can feel like progress.

You explain the plan. You announce when you are starting. You picture the result. For a moment, it feels like you moved forward.

But the goal does not respond to intention. It responds to completed actions.

Use this rule:

1. Choose the next specific action.
2. Complete it before you announce it.
3. Let repetition become your proof.

You do not need to explain the entire plan today. Complete one workout. Prepare one meal. Take one walk. Keep one promise.

Less announcing. More evidence.

Discipline - Accountability - Consistency.
