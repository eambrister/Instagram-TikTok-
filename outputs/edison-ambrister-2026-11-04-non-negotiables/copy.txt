THE NON-NEGOTIABLES

01 THE STANDARD | FIT PEOPLE / AREN'T MORE / MOTIVATED. | THEY HAVE / RULES. | 6 non-negotiables they keep on good days and bad ones.
02 NON-NEGOTIABLE 01 | PROTEIN AT / EVERY MEAL. | It keeps you full longer and helps you keep muscle while you lose fat. Put the protein on the plate first, then build around it.
03 NON-NEGOTIABLE 02 | TRAINING IS / SCHEDULED. | It goes in the calendar like an appointment. If it isn't scheduled, it's a wish.
04 NON-NEGOTIABLE 03 | A MINIMUM / FOR BAD DAYS. | A short workout. Your protein. Your steps. A bad day shrinks the plan. It doesn't cancel it.
05 NON-NEGOTIABLE 04 | NEVER / MISS TWICE. | One missed day is life. Two in a row is the start of a new habit. The day after a miss matters most.
06 NON-NEGOTIABLE 05 | SLEEP IS PART / OF THE PLAN. | Short sleep makes hunger harder to manage and training harder to recover from. Keep a consistent bedtime.
07 NON-NEGOTIABLE 06 | THEY TRACK / SOMETHING. | Food, workouts, steps or the weekly weight trend. What gets written down gets noticed, and what gets noticed gets fixed.
08 WHICH ONE / ARE YOU | MISSING? | Comment the number. Then make it non-negotiable this week.
