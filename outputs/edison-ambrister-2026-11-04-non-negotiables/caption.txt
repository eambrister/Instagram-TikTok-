Fit people aren't more motivated than you.
They have rules they don't renegotiate.

Motivation decides what you do on good days. Non-negotiables decide what you do on every other day. Here are 6 worth stealing:

1. Protein at every meal.
It keeps you full longer and helps you keep muscle while you lose fat.

2. Training is scheduled.
It goes in the calendar like an appointment. If it isn't scheduled, it's a wish.

3. A minimum for bad days.
A short workout, your protein, your steps. A bad day shrinks the plan. It doesn't cancel it.

4. Never miss twice.
One missed day is life. Two in a row is the start of a new habit.

5. Sleep is part of the plan.
Short sleep makes hunger harder to manage and training harder to recover from.

6. They track something.
Food, workouts, steps or the weekly weight trend. What gets written down gets noticed, and what gets noticed gets fixed.

You don't need all six by Monday. You need one you will not break this week.

Which one are you missing? Comment the number.
