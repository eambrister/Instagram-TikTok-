# Nutrition notes

Values are rounded reference estimates per 100 grams. They are intended for general education rather than individualized medical nutrition advice. Exact values vary with brand, cut, cooking method, water loss and added ingredients.

## Protein

- Cooked skinless chicken breast: approximately 31g protein
- Canned light tuna in water, drained: approximately 26g protein
- Cooked lean beef: approximately 26g protein
- Hard-boiled whole egg: approximately 13g protein
- Cottage cheese: approximately 11g protein

## Carbohydrates

- Dry rolled oats: approximately 68g carbohydrate
- Cooked pasta: approximately 31g carbohydrate
- Cooked white rice: approximately 28g carbohydrate
- Raw banana: approximately 23g carbohydrate
- Baked potato: approximately 21g carbohydrate

## Fiber

- Cooked black beans: approximately 9g fiber
- Cooked lentils: approximately 8g fiber
- Raw avocado: approximately 7g fiber
- Raw raspberries: approximately 7g fiber
- Cooked broccoli: approximately 3g fiber

The estimates align with typical USDA FoodData Central entries. For client tracking, use the package label or verified database entry matching the food actually consumed.
