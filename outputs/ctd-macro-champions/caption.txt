STOP FEARING FOOD. LEARN WHAT IT DOES.

Protein supports muscle retention, recovery and fullness.

Carbohydrates fuel training, movement and performance. They are not automatically responsible for fat gain. Consistently eating more total energy than your body uses is what drives weight gain over time.

Fiber helps make meals more filling and supports digestion.

You do not need a complicated diet. Build most meals around:

• A dependable protein source
• A carbohydrate portion that matches your activity and calorie target
• Fruit or vegetables for volume, fiber and micronutrients

The numbers shown are approximate. Preparation method, water content, fat percentage and brand can change the values. Use your food label and track the version you actually eat.
