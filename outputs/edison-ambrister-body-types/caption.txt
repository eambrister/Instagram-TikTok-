Your body type isn't fixed. It's two dials.

Dial one: body fat. Dial two: muscle.

Every body on this chart is the same frame at different levels. Low body fat with little muscle looks skinny. Higher body fat with a lot of muscle looks big and powerful. Most people sit somewhere in the middle.

Want to move up the chart? Lower your body fat with a steady calorie deficit and enough protein.
Want to move right? Build muscle with progressive training and enough food to recover.

You don't need a new body. You need to turn the dials.
