42 minutes. 560 reps. No equipment.

Calisthenics endurance, rotating EMOM:
Minute 1: 10 push-ups
Minute 2: 10 burpees
Minute 3: 20 squats
Repeat for 14 rounds.

That's 140 push-ups, 140 burpees and 280 squats. Every rep you finish early is rest you earn before the next minute starts.

Scale it to your level: fewer reps, fewer rounds, or knee push-ups. Just don't stop the clock.
