You're not stuck.
You're in the middle.

Most people quit fat loss during a flat week, not a bad one. The scale stops moving, it feels like nothing is working, and the plan gets thrown out right before it pays off.

Here's what's actually happening:

Water moves faster than fat. Salt, carbs, stress, sleep and a hard training day can all move the scale overnight. None of that is fat.

Flat weeks are normal. Fat can keep coming off while water hides it on the scale. Stay consistent and the trend usually shows up.

So stop judging your progress by one morning. Track these instead:
- Your weekly average weight
- Your waist measurement
- Progress photos every 2–4 weeks
- Your strength in the gym

And don't change the plan every week. If your weekly average and your waist haven't moved in 2–3 weeks, make one small change: a little less food or more daily steps. Not before.

The people who get there didn't have better weeks. They kept going through the flat ones.

Save this for the next flat week. You'll need it more then than you do today.
