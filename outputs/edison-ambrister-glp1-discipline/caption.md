My problem with GLP-1s isn't the medication. It's what it skips.

A GLP-1 lowers your appetite. You eat less, you're in a calorie deficit, you lose weight. That part works.

But it doesn't build the discipline, the habits or the mindset to keep eating that way when the shot stops. In the STEP 1 extension study, people who came off semaglutide regained about two-thirds of the weight they'd lost within a year.

If you use one, that's your call. Work with your doctor. I'd still build the habits alongside it:
• Know your calories
• Hit your protein
• Learn to say no to food you don't need

That's how I lost 80 lb and kept it off. Discipline is the part nobody can inject.

Not medical advice. Talk to your doctor about any medication.
