## Fact-check (searched November 2026)

Most of the story is supported by major outlets:
- The relationship investigation and Huang's admission in a **Nov 8, 2026** letter to staff saying he was "unfaithful to my wife".
- The alleged intelligence links, which China denies.
- His resignation, announced **Nov 12, 2026**.

Four lines don't match the reporting. These are real allegations about real people, so I recommend cutting or fixing them:

| Raw time | You say | What the reporting says | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2:05 | "unfaithful to his wife **for 40 years**" | His letter says he was unfaithful. Nothing reported says it lasted 40 years. | Cut "for 40 years". The sentence ends cleanly on "unfaithful to his wife." |
| 2:17–2:30 | "affairs with not one, not two, but **three** women. **Both** of the women were said to be working at NVIDIA." | Reports describe **two** relationships: one with a Megaspeed executive (not an NVIDIA employee) and a reported one with a senior NVIDIA executive. | Cut both sentences, plus the "here's the crazy part" lead-in, or re-record: "More reports came out about a second relationship, with an NVIDIA executive." |
| 1:14 | "an **American** businesswoman" | Reported as a **Chinese national**, an executive at Megaspeed (a Singapore company). | Re-record the line: "…had a close relationship with a businesswoman alleged to be a Chinese spy." Cutting the one word would leave "with an businesswoman". |
| 2:34 | "$500 billion wiped out **basically overnight**" | About $490–550B over **several trading days** (Nov 9–13). | Cut "basically overnight". It ends cleanly on "from NVIDIA." |

Two smaller ones (optional):
- **3:04, Tiger Woods "lost billions of dollars in sponsors."** The billions estimate is the value lost by his *sponsors'* shareholders. His own lost endorsements were in the tens of millions a year. Safe fix: cut "billions of dollars in" so it says "he lost sponsors."
- **1:10, "at the end of October, reports came out."** The public reports and the investigation came out around Nov 8. Safer: cut "At the end of October" and start the sentence on "Reports came out…".

The Easterbrook ($105M+ returned), Krzanich (2018) and Hurd (2010) lines match the public record.
