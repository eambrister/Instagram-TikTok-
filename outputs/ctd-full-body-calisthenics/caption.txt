NO GYM DOES NOT MEAN NO WORKOUT.

Complete 3 rounds:

1. Pull-ups — 6–10 reps
2. Push-ups — 10–20 reps
3. Bodyweight squats — 15–25 reps
4. Dips — 6–12 reps
5. Reverse lunges — 8–12 reps per leg
6. Glute bridges — 15–20 reps
7. Burpees — 6–10 reps
8. Hollow hold — 20–30 seconds

Rest 60–90 seconds between rounds. Use controlled reps and stop the set when your technique begins to break down.

BEGINNER OPTIONS:
• Assisted pull-ups or bodyweight rows
• Elevated push-ups
• Bench-assisted dips
• Reverse lunges while holding a stable surface
• Step-back burpees without the jump
• Bent-knee hollow hold

Make the repetitions easier when necessary. Do not make the promise to yourself optional.

Save this workout and execute it this week.
