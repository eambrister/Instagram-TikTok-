My protein tier list. Agree or disagree?

I ranked these by two things: how much protein you get for the calories, and how easy they are to eat every week.

S tier: chicken breast, eggs, lean beef, Greek yogurt, salmon.
A tier: tuna, turkey breast, shrimp, cottage cheese, white fish.
B tier: lentils, black beans, tofu, edamame, lean pork. Good protein, but beans and lentils come with more carbs per gram of protein.
C tier: deli meat, protein bars, fried chicken, sausage, cheese. Not banned, but you pay for the protein in fat, sugar or sodium.

Build most of your meals from S and A, use B to fill gaps, and keep C occasional.
