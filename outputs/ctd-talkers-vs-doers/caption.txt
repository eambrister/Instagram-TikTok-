TOMORROW HAS HEARD ENOUGH OF YOUR PLANS.

You keep saying you're going to start.
Tomorrow. Monday. Next month. When life calms down.

Be honest: how many times have you repeated that sentence while your goal stayed exactly where it was?

Talking can make you feel productive because you get the emotion of progress without paying the price of action. But your body cannot use your intentions. Your goals cannot count your explanations. The only thing that changes your life is what you complete.

The people who are serious do not need a perfect announcement. They take the first imperfect step. They train before confidence arrives. They prepare the meal before motivation shows up. They let the evidence speak later.

So close this post and do one thing now:

10 squats.
A 10-minute walk.
Prepare one meal.
Schedule tomorrow's workout.

Pick one. Finish it. Then come back.

Discipline - Accountability - Consistency.
