# No half-rep pull-ups

Reel: `pullup-form-v1.mp4` (1080 × 1920, 52 s). Pillar: **EDISON TRAINS** (form). Edison posts this one himself.

**TikTok title:** If your chin doesn't clear the bar, it doesn't count

```
Please. No more half-rep pull-ups.

A full rep:
• Start from a dead hang. Arms locked out at the bottom.
• Pull until your chin clears the bar.
• Control it back down to lockout, every rep.

Half reps feel like more work for less effort. Full reps actually build the strength.

Fewer clean reps beat more sloppy ones. Every time.
```
