# Mindset race: production notes

An original animated Reel in the "race of mindsets" format, from a reference Edison sent (an Instagram Reel showing neon stick figures racing as mindsets). The characters, script, track, day gates and sound here are our own, built procedurally by `tools/build_ea_mindset_race.py`. There's no footage and no AI generation.

## The story (29.3 s)

- **0–3 s:** WHICH ONE ARE YOU? Four runners line up (MOTIVATION, EGO, DISCIPLINE, TALENT), then the 3-2-1-GO countdown.
- **3–22.8 s:** The race. A day counter (DAY 1 → 365) tracks Discipline's distance, and gates pass overhead at DAY 7, 30, 90, 180 and 365.
  - **Motivation:** "LET'S GOOO!" → "I'll go hard Monday." → "...I'm done." Slows down and sits on the track.
  - **Ego:** "CAMERA ON ME." → "who's the slow guy?" → "NOT LIKE THIS!" Burns out and drops to its knees (impact hit).
  - **Talent:** "natural." → "I don't even need to train." → "wait... why is it hard now?" Coasts to a stop, hands on knees.
  - **Discipline:** "same pace." → "same pace." → "one more day." → "again tomorrow." Crosses DAY 365.
- **23.2–26.8 s:** DISCIPLINE DOESN'T SPRINT. / IT JUST DOESN'T STOP. The camera stops while Discipline keeps running into the distance.
- **26.8–29.3 s:** CTD end card.

## Look

Night stadium in brand tones: obsidian, gold Discipline, crimson Ego, amber Motivation, steel Talent. Pseudo-3D perspective with additive neon glow. Barlow Condensed for titles, Montserrat for the speech bubbles. Bubbles stack automatically so they never overlap.

## Sound

Original 120 BPM synth run in A minor (pads, kick, hats, clap, bass) that drops out at the finish. Countdown beeps, bubble pops, gate whooshes, a finish chime, and impacts on Ego's collapse and the final line.

−14.3 LUFS, −3.7 dBTP.
