Your back isn't just lats. And you don't need a gym to train it.

If your back training is a few pull-ups and nothing else, you're leaving most of it untrained. Here's the full picture with just a bar and a sturdy table:

The muscles: traps, rear delts, rhomboids, lats, teres major and the erector spinae along your spine.

How often: 1–2 sessions a week if you're new, 2 for most people, 2–3 only if you're recovering well. Split it into a width day (pull-ups, chin-ups, negatives) and a thickness day (inverted rows, prone Y and T raises, supermans).

How to progress without weights: assisted pull-ups, then negatives, then strict pull-ups, then slow-tempo or archer pull-ups. Use the hardest version where the last 2–3 reps are hard but clean. Hit the target on every set, then move up a step.
