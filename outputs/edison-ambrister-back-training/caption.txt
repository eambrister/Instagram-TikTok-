Your back isn't just lats.

If your back day is pulldowns and nothing else, you're leaving most of it untrained. Here's the full picture:

The muscles: traps, rear delts, rhomboids, lats, teres major and the erector spinae along your spine.

How often: 1–2 sessions a week if you're new, 2 for most people, 2–3 only if you're recovering well. Split it into a width day (pull-ups, pulldowns) and a thickness day (rows, rear delts, shrugs, back extensions).

How heavy: pick a weight where the last 2–3 reps are hard but your form stays clean. If your form breaks, it's too heavy. If you could do 5 more easy reps, it's too light.

Train it again when the soreness is mostly gone and your lifts are back to normal.
