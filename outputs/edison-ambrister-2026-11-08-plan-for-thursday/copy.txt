A PLAN FOR THURSDAY

01 EXECUTION SYSTEMS | MONDAY, YOU'RE / MOTIVATED. | THURSDAY / DECIDES. | Plan your week tonight, so it survives the day you don't feel like it.
02 MOTIVATION IS A MOOD. | A PLAN IS A / DECISION. | Motivation is high on Monday and gone by midweek. A plan doesn't depend on how you feel.
03 STEP 01 | PICK YOUR / TRAINING TIMES. | Choose the days and the exact times. Put them in your calendar like appointments you can't move.
04 STEP 02 | DECIDE YOUR / MEALS NOW. | Choose 2 or 3 meals you'll repeat this week and shop for them today. Fewer decisions, fewer slips.
05 STEP 03 | SET YOUR / MINIMUM DAY. | Decide what still counts on a bad day: a short workout, your protein and your steps.
06 STEP 04 | PLAN THE / HARD MOMENT. | Name when you usually fall off: late shifts, Friday night, the weekend. Decide what you'll do instead before it arrives.
07 A PLAN WRITTEN ON SUNDAY | BEATS / MOTIVATION / ON MONDAY.
08 SAVE THIS | FOR SUNDAY | NIGHT. | Use it to plan your week, every week.
