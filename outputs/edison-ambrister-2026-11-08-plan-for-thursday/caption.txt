Monday, you're motivated.
Thursday decides.

Most weeks don't fall apart on Monday. They fall apart midweek, when the motivation is gone and nothing was decided in advance.

Motivation is a mood. A plan is a decision. Make yours tonight:

1. Pick your training times.
Choose the days and the exact times. Put them in your calendar like appointments you can't move.

2. Decide your meals now.
Choose 2 or 3 meals you'll repeat this week and shop for them today. Fewer decisions, fewer slips.

3. Set your minimum day.
Decide what still counts on a bad day: a short workout, your protein and your steps.

4. Plan the hard moment.
Name when you usually fall off: late shifts, Friday night, the weekend. Decide what you'll do instead before it arrives.

Ten minutes tonight saves the week you'd otherwise restart next Monday.

A plan written on Sunday beats motivation on Monday.

Save this and use it to plan your week, every week.
