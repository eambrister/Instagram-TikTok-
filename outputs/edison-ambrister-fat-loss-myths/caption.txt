You're not undisciplined.
You might just be following the wrong rules.

A lot of fat-loss advice sounds strict, so it feels like it must be right. But strict and effective aren't the same thing.

5 rules that keep disciplined people stuck:

1. "Eat as little as possible."
Crash diets make you hungrier, weaker in training and more likely to quit. A moderate deficit you can hold for months wins.

2. "Carbs make you fat."
A calorie surplus does. Rice, potatoes and oats can fit. Watch the portion and what you add to it.

3. "If it says protein, it's a protein source."
Read the label. Many bars and snacks get more of their calories from sugar and fat. Build meals around chicken, eggs, tuna or lean beef.

4. "Weekends don't count."
They're 2 days out of 7. Plan one meal out, keep protein high and keep moving.

5. "The scale went up, so it's not working."
Daily weight moves with water, salt, carbs and digestion. Weigh in each morning and judge the weekly average.

Fat loss was never about finding a harder rule. It's about repeating the right basics long enough to see them work.
