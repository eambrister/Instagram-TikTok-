PRODUCTION NOTES - Fat-loss myths that keep you stuck

Kit: Monumental v1.0, condensed headline variant (chosen for the whole campaign because every myth slide carries a quote, a reply and body copy).
Canvas: 1080 x 1350, sRGB PNG. 8 slides; 5 myths. The slide count is 8 rather than the kit's default 9 because the two bedroom mirror photos share one diptych slide.

IMAGERY MODE
Exact-photo mode. Edison's pixels are not reshaped, retouched, smoothed or regenerated. No AI image generation was used.
A person-segmentation mask (MediaPipe selfie_multiclass_256x256, run locally via ai-edge-litert) separates Edison from the background so only the background is graded: desaturated, deepened and cooled. Edison receives only a light global balance (+5% contrast, slightly warm).
No sculpture, architecture or other scenery was added. Every pixel comes from the supplied photos.
Slides 04 and 08 fill the left side with a blurred, darkened copy of the same photo so the text sits on a dark photographic surface.
Slide 02: the other gym's painted pillar slogan and logo are deepened into shadow so they don't appear in Edison's post.
Slide 03: cropped to exclude two bystanders.

TYPE
Headlines: Anton (open-licence substitute; condensed variant of the kit). Body: Montserrat Medium. Utility/footer: Montserrat Bold.
These are substitutes, not the licensed Heading Now / Gotham.

CHECKS PERFORMED
- Text placement was checked automatically against the subject mask: 0% of text pixels on Edison for slides 01-07. Slide 08 reports about 36%, but that comes from the blurred background copy of the portrait behind the text, not from Edison himself; confirmed by visual inspection.
- Contact sheet and full-size inspection of slides 02, 07 and 08; footer readability fixed with a bottom gradient after the first render.
- Dimensions, folios 01/08-08/08, footer wording, caption length (1,661 characters, 5 hashtags, 21 search tags).

CLAIMS
General nutrition education only. No statistics, testimonials, client results, prices or offers. No personal transformation numbers (Edison asked to avoid another transformation post).
The close commits Edison to replying to comments with advice.
