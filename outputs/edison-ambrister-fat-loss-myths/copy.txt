01 HOOK
EDISON EATS
YOU'RE FOLLOWING / THE WRONG RULES.
5 fat-loss myths that keep disciplined people stuck, and what to do instead.

02 MYTH 01
"EAT AS LITTLE AS POSSIBLE."
SUSTAINABLE BEATS EXTREME.
Crash diets drive up hunger, drain your training and make muscle harder to keep. Choose a deficit you can hold for months, not one you quit in two weeks.

03 MYTH 02
"CARBS MAKE YOU FAT."
A SURPLUS DOES.
Fat loss comes from eating fewer calories than you burn. Rice, potatoes and oats can fit. Watch the portion, and what you pour on top.

04 MYTH 03
"IF IT SAYS PROTEIN, IT'S A PROTEIN SOURCE."
READ THE LABEL.
Many bars and snacks get more of their calories from sugar and fat than from protein. Build meals around chicken, eggs, tuna or lean beef first.

05 MYTH 04
"WEEKENDS DON'T COUNT."
THEY'RE 2 DAYS OUT OF 7.
Two unplanned days can undo five planned ones. Plan one meal out, keep your protein high and keep moving.

06 MYTH 05
"THE SCALE WENT UP. IT'S NOT WORKING."
JUDGE THE TREND.
Daily weight moves with water, salt, carbs and digestion. Weigh in each morning and compare weekly averages.

07 CLIMAX
STOP CHASING HACKS.
REPEAT THE BASICS.
A deficit you can sustain. Protein at every meal. Training you repeat. Weeks you don't write off.
