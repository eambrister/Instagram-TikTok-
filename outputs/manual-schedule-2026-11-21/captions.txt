How to hit 120g+ protein without trying hard.

One simple day:

Breakfast: 3 eggs, wholegrain toast, cottage cheese. About 34g.
Lunch: chicken and quinoa bowl. About 41g.
Snack: Greek yogurt and berries. About 20g.
Dinner: salmon and broccoli. About 39g.

That's roughly 134g, from normal food, four times a day.

The trick isn't a perfect plan. It's putting a protein at the centre of every meal.

Foods for focus, without the hype.

Eggs: protein and choline.
