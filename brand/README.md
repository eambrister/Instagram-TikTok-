# Edison Ambrister production standards

Use these preferences for future carousel work. They record the user's decisions; the brief for a particular post may specify a different campaign palette or tone.

- Canvas: 1080 × 1350 px, 4:5. Do not resize older published sets unless requested.
- Brand name: **Edison Ambrister**. Edison is the brand; do not present the content as a separate company named Cut Throat Discipline.
- Primary logo: `assets/edison-ambrister/edison-ambrister-horizontal.png`.
- Compact mark: `assets/edison-ambrister/ea-monogram.png`.
- Profile mark: `assets/edison-ambrister/edison-ambrister-profile.png`.
- The former Cut Throat Discipline emblem is retained only for historical output compatibility and must not appear on new work.
- Footer: **DISCIPLINE - ACCOUNTABILITY - CONSISTENCY**.
- Requested typography: Heading Now 31–38 Bold for headlines, Gotham for supporting copy. Licensed files have not been supplied.
- Available temporary typography: Barlow Condensed ExtraBold plus Montserrat Medium/Bold; open font files and OFL licenses are in `fonts/`. Describe substitutes accurately.
- Headline treatment: strong condensed uppercase; body copy must stay readable on phones. Keep logo, numbering and safe margins consistent.
- Cinematic imagery should illustrate the individual slide's message. Avoid repetitive scenes, invented performance statistics and generated testimonial evidence.
- Content: discipline, accountability, consistency and fitness education. Do not introduce coaching offer posts until the user finalizes the offer and requests them.
- Delivery: numbered PNGs, overview preview, caption, transcript and posting ZIP, published to `eambrister/Instagram-TikTok-` on `main` under the authorization already given in this conversation.

The burpees campaign uses the supplied brief's obsidian, titanium silver, deep crimson and warm white palette while preserving the established logo.

The full design framework, format families, grid, copy structure and quality-control checklist are in [`CONTENT_SYSTEM.md`](CONTENT_SYSTEM.md). Machine-readable values are in [`tokens.json`](tokens.json), and reusable Edison Ambrister transparent overlays are in [`templates/`](templates/).
