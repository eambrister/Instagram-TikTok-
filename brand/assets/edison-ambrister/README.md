# Edison Ambrister logo system

The personal brand replaces the Cut Throat Discipline name while preserving the established gold, silver, crimson and obsidian visual language.

## Files

- `edison-ambrister-horizontal.png` / `.svg` — primary logo for posts, websites and video headers
- `edison-ambrister-stacked.png` / `.svg` — centered layouts, covers and end cards
- `ea-monogram.png` / `.svg` — compact mark for small placements and watermarks
- `edison-ambrister-profile.png` / `.svg` — square social profile image on obsidian

## Usage

- Prefer the horizontal full-name logo whenever it remains legible.
- Use the EA monogram for compact placements, watermarks and favicons.
- Use the square profile asset for Instagram, TikTok and other profile images.
- Maintain clear space around the logo equal to at least 10% of the mark’s height.
- Do not recolor, stretch, rotate or separate the monogram elements.
- The crimson slash represents decisive action and continuity with the previous brand’s intensity.
- The footer remains `DISCIPLINE - ACCOUNTABILITY - CONSISTENCY`.
