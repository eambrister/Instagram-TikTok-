# Edison Ambrister: Monumental kit

This is the second brand kit. It **does not replace** the core system in [`../CONTENT_SYSTEM.md`](../CONTENT_SYSTEM.md). The core kit stays the default for every post. Use this kit only when a brief asks for "Monumental", "cinematic V3" or "the second kit", or when a topic clearly suits it (see "When to use it").

It translates four uploaded guides into Edison's brand. The originals are kept in [`source-guides/`](source-guides/):

| File | What it contributes |
|---|---|
| `carousel-baseline-v1.pdf` | Full-bleed photography, tall condensed type, aged-gold hierarchy, the layout grammar and the climax slide |
| `universal-cinematic-carousel-v3.pdf` | Editorial serif headlines, the six-composition library, the copy engine, the compositing and repair rules |
| `monumental-standard-v3.pdf` | Sculpture and architecture as metaphor, the emotional engine, the nine-slide arc, the identity-protection rules |
| `caption-system-v1.pdf` | The caption rules for this kit, summarised in [`CAPTION_SYSTEM.md`](CAPTION_SYSTEM.md) |

The reference campaigns belong to another creator ("The Operator Standard", "Train. Trade. Transform.", "Operator Mentorship", "T³"). **Never reuse their people, slogans, marks, offers, prices, DM keywords or claims.** Take the craft only.

## 1. When to use it

| Use the Monumental kit for | Keep the core kit for |
|---|---|
| Identity, standards and mindset carousels (THE STANDARD) | Workouts and exercise technique (EDISON TRAINS) |
| Personal-story and reflection posts built on real photos of Edison (MY PROOF) | Food comparisons, macro guides and other infographics (EDISON EATS) |
| Behaviour and accountability lessons that suit a cinematic, editorial tone (EXECUTION SYSTEMS) | Typography-only statements and checklists |
| Occasional "campaign" pieces meant to stand out in the feed | Reels (this kit is for still images only) |

**Hard requirement: real photographs of Edison.** This kit is built on real portraits. Without supplied photos of Edison, don't generate a person to stand in for him. Use a slide with no person in it (layout D) or switch to the core kit.

## 2. Fixed engine vs brand layer

From V3 §03, "Separate engine from brand":

- **Fixed engine:** one dominant idea per slide, a headline → body → utility hierarchy, subject protection, the spacing grid, clear contrast, coherent photo compositing, pacing by slide role, honest evidence, and a repeatable review and export workflow.
- **Edison's brand layer:** the Edison Ambrister name and logo, the footer wording, his story (222 → ~142 lb, described as personal experience only), his audience and his voice. Everything below is set for him.

**Adaptation mode:** *Faithful V3*: serif headlines, a dark base and warm metallic atmosphere. Choose the condensed variant (§5) per campaign, never per slide.

## 3. Canvas

Edison prefers 4:5, so this kit defaults to **1080 × 1350** and recomposes the guides' 1080 × 1080 layouts vertically, as V3 §16 permits. Produce **1080 × 1080** only when a brief asks for square.

| Zone | 1080 × 1350 (default) | 1080 × 1080 (on request) |
|---|---|---|
| Essential copy safe area | x 80–1000, y 80–1270 | x 80–1000, y 80–1000 |
| Footer / folio band | y 1236–1272 | y 968–998 |
| Optional fine frame inset | 28–48 px from the edge | 28–48 px from the edge |
| Gap between text and the subject's silhouette | 32–56 px | 32–56 px |

- The photograph always runs **full bleed** to all four edges. No cream mat, card, rounded corners or header bar.
- Spacing uses 8 px steps: at least 8 px of clear space between large glyphs; 28–48 px from headline to body; 20–32 px between paragraphs; 48–80 px between major blocks.
- Use a 12-column mental grid inside the safe area: 5–6 columns for a text block beside a portrait, 8–10 for a full-width statement. Don't mix alignments within one reading block.

## 4. Palette

Colour comes mostly from the photograph. The kit adds a dark reading surface, bone text and one gold accent. Hex values are production targets inferred from the references.

| Role | Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Base | Deep charcoal | `#080909` | Dominant reading surface and scrims |
| Shadow | Ink | `#101010` | Architectural shadow, gradients |
| Primary type | Bone | `#EEE9DF` | Setup lines and body copy |
| Accent | Antique gold | `#BE995C` | The one decisive phrase; rules |
| Highlight | Light gold | `#D9BC78` | Restrained highlights and gradient tops on gold type |
| Utility | Stone | `#B6B0A5` | Footer, folio, quiet labels |
| Atmosphere (optional) | Storm teal | `#253A40` | Cool shadow tint in outdoor or stormy grades |

Rules:

- **Bone carries the setup and gold carries the reversal.** Emphasise one idea per slide.
- Gold should read like aged metal or ink, never bright yellow neon. A subtle texture inside large gold letters is fine; body text stays clean.
- **No crimson and no violet.** Crimson belongs to the core kit. Violet was the reference creator's product accent.
- Don't recolour skin or clothing to match the palette.
- Contrast targets, checked on the final image: 4.5:1 for small essential text, 3:1 for large display text. Fix a problem with a feathered local scrim behind the type, not by darkening the whole photo.

## 5. Typography

| Role | Default (editorial serif) | Condensed variant | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hero / headline | **Bodoni Moda Black** (`brand/fonts/BodoniModa-Black.ttf`) | **Anton** (`brand/fonts/Anton-Regular.ttf`) | Uppercase. Bone setup, one gold line |
| Secondary display | Bodoni Moda Bold | Anton | |
| Body / support | Montserrat Medium | Montserrat Medium | Sentence case |
| Utility / footer / labels | Montserrat Bold, tracked +2 to +4 | Same | Uppercase |

- These are open substitutes, so say so in production notes. The reference lettering came from an image generator and can't be matched exactly. Bodoni Moda is **wider** than the compressed Didone in the references, so break headlines into shorter lines rather than squeezing them.
- Never stretch or compress lettering to make it fit. No hard outlines, bevels, 3D extrusion or a glow around every letter.

Type scale (px):

| Element | 1080 × 1350 | 1080 × 1080 |
|---|---|---|
| Exceptional short climax | 132–188 | 132–160 |
| Headline | 84–126 | 84–126 |
| Secondary display | 48–72 | 48–72 |
| Body (never below 28 for essential reading) | 30–36 | 30–36 |
| Utility / footer | 18–22 | 18–22 |

Leading: headlines 0.92–1.05 × the font size; body 1.25–1.4 ×. Tracking: display −1 to +1 px; uppercase labels +2 to +3 px. Break lines at units of meaning, and never leave an accidental one-word last line. Measure rendered glyph bounds and don't trust guessed heights.

## 6. Brand marks on every slide

- **Logo:** the official Edison Ambrister artwork only, from `brand/assets/edison-ambrister/`. In this kit it sits **lower-left**, where the reference campaigns placed their mark. Use the horizontal lockup when it stays legible at 72–100 px wide. Otherwise use the EA monogram at 56–72 px. Never redraw it, recolour it or place it over the subject.
- **Footer / folio:** lower-right in the footer band, Montserrat Bold, Stone, tracked:
  `DISCIPLINE · ACCOUNTABILITY · CONSISTENCY  ·  03/09`
  The wording is the core brand footer, so it must never change. Folios run in sequence, and every slide shows the correct total.
- Optional: a thin antique-gold rule (1–2 px) between the logo and the footer, or under a headline group.

## 7. Composition library

There are eight families: A–F from V3 and G–H from the Baseline. The zones below are planning bounds for 1080 × 1350. Never mirror Edison to make a layout work. Choose the photo first, then the layout.

| ID | Name | Planning zones (1080 × 1350) | Best for | Words |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | Presence hook | Subject x 510–1040; type x 80–550, headline y 380–850, support below. Background metaphor behind or above the type | A short identity hook or sharp opening claim | 12–30 |
| **B** | Mirror statement | Subject x 60–450; copy x 475–995, vertically centred | Personal standards, preparation, a close detail | 25–45 |
| **C** | Diptych | Hero photo left 45–50%; copy upper-right y 110–640; second photo lower-right y 680–1230; a clean diagonal or straight divider | Two photos explaining one relationship (training + meal prep, before + now) | 25–45 |
| **D** | Threshold | Centred text x 140–940, y 110–560; doorway or path focal point below, framed by edges | A visual reset after asymmetrical slides; slides with no person | 8–25 |
| **E** | Journey | Text left x 80–590, y 120–740; full-body subject right | Reflection, process, slowing the narrative | 25–50 |
| **F** | Invitation | Portrait right x 570–1040; copy left x 80–580: title y 250–550, support y 610–925, action y 975–1110. Optional fine frame | The close: one reason, one action | 20–50 |
| **G** | Typographic climax | Full-width headline x 60–1020, y 56–575; bone setup above one dominant gold line; subject lower-centre | The single biggest statement in the set (use once) | 12–35 |
| **H** | Right-side manifesto | Subject x 55–570; text column x 630–1000, y 190–1125. Small setup, stacked gold list, compact bone challenge | List-like declarations | 12–35 |

**Non-collision rule:** zones are a starting point, not permission to cover a person. Keep text off eyes, face, hands and key physique areas. If copy doesn't fit at a readable size, change the crop or the layout. If it still doesn't fit, split the slide. Never shrink body copy into microtype.

## 8. Photography, identity and atmosphere

**Protect the human. This is the most important rule in the kit.**

- Use only photos Edison supplies. Preserve his face, expression, gaze, hairline, skin tone and texture, body proportions, muscularity, tattoos, clothing, accessories, hands and pose. Scaling the whole photo is fine. Reshaping body parts, smoothing skin, deepening abs, widening shoulders or whitening teeth is not.
- **Exact-photo mode (default):** keep Edison's original pixels on a protected masked layer and edit only the surroundings. **AI-assisted composite mode** is allowed only when disclosed in production notes and checked side by side against the source. If his likeness drifts, go back to the original photo and never use a flawed render as the new source.
- No people from the reference PDFs, no invented second people, no new angles of Edison invented from an existing photo, no statue or marble texture over Edison himself.
- Crop out phone chrome, playback controls, likes and usernames from screenshots. Never crop through a wrist, knee or fingertip just to enlarge the face.

**Grade:** balance exposure and white balance → mask the subject → calm distracting background highlights → add warm light that has a believable source, or cooler shadow separation → set contrast → add fine grain last. Make it deeper, not darker. Avoid orange skin, crushed blacks, HDR halos, artificial muscle definition, heavy vignettes and fog over half the frame.

**Symbols (optional, at most one per slide):** each prop must have a job.

| Motif | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Doorway | Access, decision, transition |
| Stone bust / sculpture | Study, standards, legacy |
| Path / corridor | Continuation, consistency |
| Reflective metal / mirror | Precision, self-assessment |
| Steam | Recovery, quiet |
| Diagonal divider | The relationship between two processes |
| Light shaft | Attention, direction |

- Keep sculpture behind or beside Edison with clear separation in depth. The statue's face must never compete with his.
- Added temples and columns are **editorial scenery**, not proof of a place visited, wealth or results. Say so in production notes. For nutrition topics, a real kitchen usually builds more trust than a temple.
- **Effect budget per slide:** one main photo, at most one large symbolic addition, and one atmospheric treatment. A two-photo diptych is the intentional exception.

**Layer order:** background environment → symbolic addition → contact shadows → protected Edison layer → local light integration → readability scrim → headline → body → rule / logo / folio → final grain.

## 9. Narrative and copy

Use the core voice: direct, disciplined, practical, never cruel. The emotional engine:

1. **Recognition:** name an observable behaviour the reader will recognise ("You restart every Monday").
2. **Tension:** show a credible cost without inventing statistics.
3. **Agency:** resolve it with a small action and a reason.
4. **Action:** one step the reader can take today.

**Hooks:** 3–10 dominant words and one support line that opens a question; 12–30 words in total. Working patterns: *repeated behaviour + cost* ("Another Monday. The same plan."), *contradiction + consequence* ("Your standards end at your mood."), *protective belief + challenge*. Test: is the tension clear in two seconds, does the next slide develop it, and could Edison say it out loud?

**Arcs by length:**

| Slides | Beats |
|---|---|
| 5 | Hook / pattern / method / implementation / close |
| 6 | Hook / standard / mechanism / application / reflection / close |
| 7 | Hook / frame / evidence / teach / mechanism / recap / close |
| **9 (default)** | 01 interrupt with the cost · 02 name the recurring behaviour · 03 what's missing · 04–06 three teaching modules (Edison's real method, not a borrowed three-pillar slogan) · 07 support mechanism (accountability) · 08 concrete implementation (e.g. a seven-day scorecard) · 09 summarise the standard plus one action |
| 10–12 | Add stakes, evidence, an objection, a worked example and a checklist. Insert a sparse reset after every dense pair |

**Rhythm:** change portrait scale, alignment or photo count every 1–3 slides. Never use one layout more than twice in a row. Use the climax (G) only once. Never put three dense slides in a row.

**Word budgets:** hook 12–30 · teaching 25–50 · mechanism 35–60 · reset 8–25 · close 20–50.

**The close:** the coaching offer is **not finalized**, so close with the takeaway, a save prompt, a comment question or a profile-follow prompt tied to the series. No price, offer slide, product boxes, DM keyword, invented benefits or urgency until Edison confirms an offer and asks for it.

## 10. Production workflow

1. **Brief:** fill in the input card (§11).
2. **Inventory:** list every photo with its filename, orientation, usable negative space and the slide it will serve. Use every photo Edison asks to be used.
3. **Storyboard:** for each slide, record the role, exact copy, photo, layout family, protected area and one optional symbol. Lock the copy before rendering.
4. **Style slide first:** render one representative slide, check it, then build the rest to the same spec.
5. **Build:** typeset as real text layers. Generators belong in `tools/build_monumental_<topic>.py`.
6. **Check:** at 100% and at about 360 px wide on a phone. Compare Edison with the source photo, proofread every word and folio, and look for collisions, clipped glyphs and repeated layouts. Fix one failure at a time.
7. **Deliver** to `outputs/edison-ambrister-<topic>/`:
   `01-hook.png … NN-close.png`, `preview.jpg` (contact sheet, outside the numbered set), `copy.txt`, `caption.txt`, `source-map.txt`, `production-notes.txt` (imagery mode, font substitutes, editorial scenery, open limitations) and `final-carousel.zip`, all sRGB.

## 12. Quality gate

A carousel isn't finished until every line passes:

- [ ] Every slide has one job, and the hook is paid off
- [ ] Copy matches `copy.txt` exactly; the folios run in sequence and show the correct total
- [ ] No invented statistics, testimonials, prices, offers, credentials or urgency
- [ ] Edison matches every source photo: face, proportions, hands, tattoos, clothing text
- [ ] No reference-campaign people, slogans or marks anywhere
- [ ] Text stays off the face, hands and key physique areas, with no collisions or clipping
- [ ] Body text is readable at phone size; contrast targets are met on the final image
- [ ] Official logo and exact footer wording on every slide
- [ ] Layouts vary; the climax is used once; the effect budget is respected
- [ ] Correct dimensions, sRGB, every file opens, ZIP tested, preview outside the numbered set
- [ ] Production notes name the font substitutes, any editorial scenery and any AI-assisted compositing

**Five-second test:** does it look like one campaign? Is Edison clearly visible? Is the main message obvious? Is there room to breathe? Would it still work without the effects?

**Acceptance order when rules conflict:** Edison's explicit instructions → accurate claims and identity → phone readability → clear narrative → consistent art direction → decorative drama.
