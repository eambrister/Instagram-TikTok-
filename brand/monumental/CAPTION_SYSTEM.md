# Monumental kit caption system

These are the caption rules for posts made with the Monumental kit, adapted from [`source-guides/caption-system-v1.pdf`](source-guides/caption-system-v1.pdf). Core-kit posts keep the caption guidance in `CLAUDE_ONBOARDING.md` §7 unless Edison asks for this system there too.

**The framework is fixed. The voice is Edison's.** The source document has no personality of its own. Every line must sound like Edison: direct, disciplined, practical, clean language.

## Output order

```
HOOK
VALUE-DRIVEN BODY
CLOSING
CTA
5 HASHTAGS
UP TO 30 SEARCH TAGS (comma-separated, no #)
```

## Core standard

- No forced profanity, no AI filler, no detached captions. The caption responds to what the carousel actually shows.
- **No invented facts:** no made-up client results, testimonials, scarcity, credentials, statistics or partnerships. Edison's 222 → ~142 lb story is his personal experience, never a promised outcome.
- Every line earns its place, and each caption has one primary objective.
- Paste test: if the caption could go under a competitor's post without anyone noticing, it isn't specific enough to Edison.

## 1. Hook (1–3 short lines)

It must work even if the reader sees nothing else. Combine at least two of: **specificity, tension, relevance, novelty, consequence.**

Types: pain, contrarian, identity, curiosity, specific result, scene.

Starter formulas. Rewrite them until they sound like Edison:

- Pain: "You're not struggling with [surface problem]. You're paying for [deeper problem]."
- Cost: "[Behaviour] is costing you more than [obvious cost]."
- Contradiction: "The thing that looks like [positive] may be creating [negative]."
- Identity: "At some point, [desired identity] has to become more than something you say."
- Educational: "Before you [action], understand these [number] things."
- Story: "[Concrete moment]. That was when I realized [unexpected lesson]."

Avoid default openers such as "Most people…", "In today's world…" and "Here's the truth…".

## 2. Value-driven body

Diagnose before you prescribe, and explain *why* as well as *what*. Use specific steps, examples and decision rules. Handle the likeliest objection. Keep paragraphs short and vary sentence length.

Pick a body framework:

| Post type | Flow |
|---|---|
| Educational | Problem → cause → framework → application → takeaway |
| Story / MY PROOF | Scene → friction → decision → result → lesson |
| Behind the scenes | What happened → why it matters → process → standard |
| Opinion / THE STANDARD | Thesis → reasoning → example → nuance → implication |

## 3. Closing

One memorable idea, not a summary. Reframe the problem, state the governing principle, or contrast the old behaviour with the new standard.

## 4. CTA (exactly one)

Match it to how warm the audience is:

- **Cold:** comment an opinion, save, share, follow for a defined series.
- **Problem-aware:** answer a diagnostic question or comment a keyword. Use a keyword only when a real resource exists.
- **Offer-aware:** book / apply / enroll. **Not used until Edison confirms the coaching offer.**

Comment prompts should start real conversation, not bait engagement. Ask for meaningful answers, never forced confessions or sensitive personal details.

The pinned-comment style from the reference, written in Edison's voice: "What's one promise to yourself you're tired of breaking? Name it. Then tell me what you're doing differently tomorrow."

## 5. Discovery layer

- **Exactly 5 hashtags:** 1 brand or category tag, 2 high-relevance niche tags, 1 audience or problem tag, and 1 contextual tag (e.g. location, `#bahamasfitness`, when it's relevant).
- Then **up to 30 comma-separated search tags without #**. Use phrases people actually search for: the problem, the solution, the topic, the audience, the location when relevant, and "edison ambrister". No trivial repeats and no unrelated high-volume terms.

## Length

- Plan to a **2,200-character hard ceiling** for the whole deliverable, including CTA and tags.
- Default target: **1,500–2,050 characters.** Cut repetition before cutting the hook, the teaching or the CTA.

## Psychological drivers (only what the post supports)

Loss awareness, identity congruence, specificity, open loops, contrast, self-recognition, agency, belonging (only where a real community exists) and proof (only genuine evidence). Never manufacture urgency, shame insecurity or imply guaranteed results.

## Quality scorecard

Score each item 0–2 silently and rewrite until the total is **18/20 or higher**: hook strength · post relevance · brand-voice fidelity · specificity · useful value · emotional resonance · readability and rhythm · credibility and claim discipline · closing strength · CTA fit.

## Anti-slop filter

Delete or rewrite any line that:

- sounds like a motivational quote account
- could apply to any industry
- repeats the previous sentence
- uses hype where evidence would be stronger
- claims the audience feels something without grounds
- uses fake authority, scarcity or certainty
- over-explains the visual
- piles up adjectives
- turns the post into a sales pitch
- sounds impressive but says nothing
