# Edison Ambrister content system

This is the default framework for every Edison Ambrister post. Edison is the brand, so content should connect the expertise, standards and lived experience directly to him. A campaign may introduce a specific visual idea, but it should still look like it came from this system.

## Brand promise

Edison Ambrister helps people replace inconsistent intention with disciplined execution. Every post should do at least one of four jobs:

1. **Teach** — give the audience something useful they can apply.
2. **Challenge** — confront an excuse or weak pattern.
3. **Prove** — show Edison's process, results or lived experience.
4. **Activate** — give the audience a workout, checklist or next action.

## Formats

### A. Cinematic carousel

Use for stories, mindset, transformations and high-emotion education.

- 1080 × 1350 pixels, 4:5
- 6–9 slides
- One cinematic image and one idea per slide
- Dark image treatment with warm gold highlights and restrained crimson
- Headline occupies roughly the upper third
- Supporting copy stays under 35 words per slide

Slide sequence:

1. Pattern-breaking hook
2. Define the problem
3. Show the cost
4. Explain why it happens
5. Introduce the principle
6. Give the method
7. Address resistance
8. Give the action
9. Closing standard or question

### B. Educational infographic

Use for workouts, nutrition comparisons, checklists and reference posts.

- 1080 × 1350 pixels, 4:5
- Cream editorial background or obsidian technical background
- Large subject or food photography
- Gold values and section markers
- Crimson used for warnings, contrasts or one strong emphasis
- Labels remain readable without zooming
- Cite assumptions such as cooked, raw, drained or per serving

### C. Typography statement

Use for hard-hitting discipline messages.

- 1080 × 1350 pixels, 4:5
- Maximum 12 words on the main slide
- Headline fills 55–70% of the useful canvas
- One highlighted phrase in gold
- Crimson may underline or strike through one phrase
- No decorative filler competing with the statement

### D. Reel

Use for comparisons, exercise demonstrations, personal process and short lessons.

- 1080 × 1920 pixels, 9:16
- Hook within the first second
- New visual information every 0.5–1.5 seconds
- Keep critical text between y=250 and y=1580
- Keep the lower 340 pixels free of essential information for Instagram controls
- Use the 9:16 overlay as the top layer

## Fixed visual rules

### Logo

- Use `assets/edison-ambrister/edison-ambrister-horizontal.png` as the primary content lockup.
- Use `assets/edison-ambrister/ea-monogram.png` only where the full name cannot remain legible.
- Use `assets/edison-ambrister/edison-ambrister-profile.png` for square profile applications.
- Default placement: upper-left.
- 4:5 size: 72–100 pixels wide.
- 9:16 size: 88–120 pixels wide.
- Maintain at least 40 pixels from the outer canvas edge.
- Never redraw, recolor, stretch or place the logo over a busy subject.

### Footer

Every designed post uses:

**DISCIPLINE - ACCOUNTABILITY - CONSISTENCY**

- Centered in Montserrat Bold, uppercase
- Silver or warm white
- Separated from the content by a thin titanium rule
- Omit only when the Reel interface makes it unreadable; the logo must remain

### Typography

Requested brand fonts:

- Headline: Heading Now 31–38 Bold
- Support: Gotham

Current installed substitutes:

- Headline: Barlow Condensed ExtraBold
- Support: Montserrat Medium/Bold

Hierarchy for 1080 × 1350:

- Hero headline: 76–104 px
- Slide headline: 58–82 px
- Subheading: 26–34 px
- Body copy: 24–30 px
- Small label: 18–22 px
- Footer: 15–18 px

Headlines are uppercase. Body copy uses sentence case unless the copy is a short directive.

## Grid

### 4:5 posts

- Canvas: 1080 × 1350
- Outer safe margin: 46 px
- Header boundary: y=140–175
- Footer boundary: y=1275–1305
- Main content area: approximately y=180–1260
- Standard gap: 24 px
- Standard corner radius: 16–20 px

### Reels

- Canvas: 1080 × 1920
- Outer safe margin: 48 px
- Header: y=40–180
- Primary text safe area: y=250–1580
- Interface-risk area: y=1580–1920

## Image direction

- Premium commercial fitness photography or realistic anatomical illustration
- Obsidian, silver and gold materials with subtle crimson detail
- Controlled directional light and readable midtones
- One clear hero subject or comparison
- Avoid generic stock-photo smiles, cartoon stick figures, excessive neon, fake sweat splashes and cluttered gym backgrounds
- Generated exercise poses must be checked for correct anatomy and movement mechanics

## Copy framework

Use this order whenever possible:

1. **Hook:** Name the tension in one sentence.
2. **Truth:** Explain what is actually happening.
3. **Mechanism:** Show why it happens.
4. **Method:** Give specific steps, quantities or examples.
5. **Standard:** Connect the lesson to discipline, accountability or consistency.
6. **Action:** Ask for a save, response or concrete next step.

Avoid claims that promise guaranteed results. Personal results should be described as personal experience, not a universal outcome.

## Quality-control checklist

Before publishing, verify:

- Correct 4:5 or 9:16 dimensions
- Official logo, correct size and clear space
- Correct footer wording
- Barlow Condensed/Montserrat until licensed fonts are supplied
- Gold is the main emphasis color
- Body text is readable at phone size
- No more than one core idea per slide
- Exercise form is anatomically credible
- Nutrition units and preparation states are stated
- No invented statistics, testimonials or client results
- Caption matches the post
- Files include final PNG/MP4, preview and caption
