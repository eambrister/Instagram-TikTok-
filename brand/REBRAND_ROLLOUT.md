# Edison Ambrister rebrand rollout

The brand is moving from a separate Cut Throat Discipline identity to a creator-led personal brand: **Edison Ambrister**.

## Positioning

**Edison Ambrister helps people build the discipline, nutrition structure and accountability needed to execute their fitness goals consistently.**

The phrase “Cut Throat Discipline” may remain part of Edison’s story or language, but it is no longer the public-facing brand name or logo.

## Visual transition

- New posts use the Edison Ambrister horizontal logo or EA monogram.
- Existing Cut Throat Discipline posts may remain live as part of the documented journey.
- Do not mix the old and new logos on the same new asset.
- Keep the established colors, fonts and footer so the transition remains recognizable.
- Use a short transition period in captions if useful: “Edison Ambrister, formerly Cut Throat Discipline.” Do not retain this indefinitely.

## Website transition

Update the website in this order:

1. Browser title and metadata
2. Header logo and brand name
3. Hero copy written in Edison’s voice
4. About section centered on Edison’s transformation
5. Social sharing image and favicon
6. Form and email language
7. Any remaining references to Cut Throat Discipline

Preserve existing URLs until redirects and analytics are understood.
