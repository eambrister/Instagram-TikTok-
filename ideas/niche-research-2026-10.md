# Niche research: what is overperforming (October 2026)

**Method:** two vidIQ outlier searches covering Instagram Reels and TikTok, July–October 2026, English captions. An "outlier" is a post that beats that creator's usual (median) views by the stated multiple. The searches were (1) discipline, consistency and fat-loss mindset and (2) fat-loss transformation and nutrition lessons. A web search on carousel performance was added.

**Limitation:** vidIQ indexes videos only, not carousels. These results show which **topics and hooks** are winning in the niche right now. They don't prove carousel performance, so treat them as topic signals and turn the winners into carousels.

Across both searches, the common thread is that a **specific personal number or rule beats general motivation**. Pure mindset talk without a method appears less often among the outliers.

## Carousel topics ranked by evidence

1. **"Things I gave up to lose 80 lbs, and keep it off."** Patterns 1 + 2. A listicle, one item per slide, over his physique photos. *(Monumental)*
2. **"222 → 142. I kept it off. Here's what I ate."** Patterns 1 + 8. His real meal rotation. Needs food photos. *(Monumental cover + core food slides)*
3. **"Fat loss isn't a straight line."** Pattern 3. The slow middle months and why people quit at the plateau. *(Monumental)*
4. **"The 7 non-negotiables that took me from 222 to 142."** Patterns 1 + 6. *(Monumental)*
5. **"Fat-loss mistakes I made so you don't have to."** Pattern 4. Use only mistakes Edison confirms he actually made. *(Core or Monumental)*
6. **"Losing weight is simple. It isn't easy."** Pattern 5: four basics plus why people still fail. *(Monumental)*
7. **"Signs your deficit is working (even when the scale won't move)."** Pattern 9. Must stay accurate and non-medical. *(Core)*
8. **"You don't need motivation. You need a plan for Thursday."** Pattern 7: the Monday Loop, reframed. *(Monumental)*

**Facts to confirm with Edison before writing #1, #2, #4 or #5:** what he actually gave up, his actual mistakes, and his rules. Never invent them.
