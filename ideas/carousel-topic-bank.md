# Carousel topic bank

A brainstorm of carousel topics for Edison Ambrister, written October 2026. It avoids topics already published in `outputs/`: talkers vs doers, burpees, the execution cycle, the 80-pound lessons, food before vs now, routine, the holiday trinity, macro champions and calisthenics.

Each topic lists a working hook, the series label, the kit that fits best, a short arc, and the photos it needs. **Monumental** topics need real photos of Edison. None of these include an offer slide, because the coaching offer isn't finalized.

Kits: **M** = [Monumental kit](../brand/monumental/STYLE_GUIDE.md) (cinematic, photo-led). **C** = [core kit](../brand/CONTENT_SYSTEM.md) (infographic / typography).

## Recommended first five

These suit the Monumental kit best and draw most directly on Edison's own story.

### 1. The Monday Loop
- **Hook:** "ANOTHER MONDAY. THE SAME PLAN." / *You don't need a fresh start. You need a plan that survives Thursday.*
- **Series / kit:** THE STANDARD · M · 9 slides
- **Arc:** the restart pattern → what restarting costs (weeks lost to "starting again") → why Monday plans fail (all-or-nothing, no fallback) → three modules: start today with a minimum day · decide your fallback workout and meal in advance · never miss twice → accountability check-in → a 7-day "minimum standard" tracker → close: "Start on the day you read this."
- **Photos:** a weekday training shot; a portrait with negative space on the left for the hook; a quiet doorway or corridor shot for the reset slide.

### 2. The 80 Pounds Didn't Leave in a Weekend
- **Hook:** "IT DIDN'T HAPPEN IN A WEEKEND." / *222 to 142. Less than two years. Mostly boring days.*
- **Series / kit:** MY PROOF · M · 7 slides
- **Arc:** the result → the unglamorous middle (months where the scale barely moved) → what repeated daily (tracking, protein, repeatable meals) → what he stopped doing → the lesson: consistency over intensity → "This is my experience, not a promise. The process is the point." → close: save it for the slow weeks.
- **Photos:** a current full-body portrait; an older photo if Edison is comfortable using one (diptych C); meal-prep photos.

### 3. Stop Negotiating With the Work
- **Hook:** "STOP NEGOTIATING WITH THE WORK." / *You already agreed to it. The debate is the delay.*
- **Series / kit:** THE STANDARD · M · 6 slides
- **Arc:** the internal bargaining ("I'll go tomorrow / I'll eat clean after this weekend") → the cost of re-deciding every day → decide-once rules (fixed training times, a default meal list, a "no zero days" floor) → reflection slide → close: "Pick one rule. Write it down tonight."
- **Photos:** a mirror or locker-room portrait (layout B); a training shot; a quiet environment.

### 4. The Weekend Problem
- **Hook:** "FIVE GOOD DAYS. TWO BAD ONES." / *Your weekdays aren't the problem.*
- **Series / kit:** EXECUTION SYSTEMS · M · 7 slides
- **Arc:** recognise the Friday-to-Sunday slide → why weekends break routines (no structure, social pressure, "I earned it") → three weekend rules: keep the wake time, pre-plan one social meal, train Saturday morning → "Enjoy the weekend. Don't donate the week to it." → close: comment your hardest weekend moment.
- **Photos:** an outdoor or relaxed portrait; a Saturday-morning training shot; a social or food setting if available.

### 5. Nobody Is Coming to Check On You
- **Hook:** "NOBODY IS COMING TO CHECK ON YOU." / *So build something that does.*
- **Series / kit:** EXECUTION SYSTEMS · M · 9 slides
- **Arc:** why private goals fade → accountability as structure, not motivation → three modules: a daily check-in (one message, three answers) · a weekly review (weight trend, sessions, what slipped) · a person or a log you report to → the support mechanism → a sample check-in template → close: save it and send it to the friend who'll hold you to it. This one sets up the future coaching offer without selling it.
- **Photos:** a phone-in-hand portrait; a training shot; a desk or planning shot for the template slide.
