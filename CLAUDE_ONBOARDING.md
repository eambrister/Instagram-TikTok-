Use this document as the operating context for helping Edison Ambrister build his personal fitness brand under his own name, **Edison Ambrister**. Read it before writing copy, proposing content, designing posts or changing repository files.

- **Creator:** Edison Ambrister

The brand helps people who struggle to execute consistently. The central message is that goals require discipline, accountability and repeated action. Content should make people confront weak patterns while giving them a useful next step.

## 2. Edison’s personal story

Edison started at **222 pounds**, lost **80 pounds in less than two years**, reached approximately **142 pounds**, and kept the weight off. He now has a lean physique and uses his own transformation as the brand’s primary proof.

Describe this as Edison’s personal experience. Never imply that every person will lose 80 pounds or achieve the same result in the same period.

His major nutrition changes were:

- Prioritizing whole foods
- Eating more fruit and vegetables
- Prioritizing protein
- Counting calories and macronutrients
- Preparing repeatable meals
- Removing many foods he found difficult to moderate

His previous meals often included large portions of pancakes or waffles with syrup, pizza, sushi, spring rolls, quesadillas, ribs, peas and rice, coleslaw, macaroni, cakes, candy and other sweets.

His current meals commonly include chicken breast, lean ground beef, tuna, eggs, rice, potatoes, oatmeal, cream of wheat, fruit and vegetables. He may skip breakfast or eat eggs, oatmeal or cream of wheat.

Do not frame individual foods as morally good or bad. Explain the effect of portions, preparation, calorie density, protein, fiber and repeated eating patterns.

## 3. Audience

The ideal audience knows they are inconsistent, undisciplined or insufficiently committed. They often:

- Start and stop fitness plans
- Delay action until Monday or tomorrow
- Rely on motivation
- Struggle with calorie control and repeatable eating
- Need external accountability
- Know what to do but do not execute consistently
- Lose momentum during weekends, holidays, travel or social events

The brand should speak directly to this behavior. Strong language is welcome when it creates useful tension. Avoid empty insults, humiliation, medical claims or shame about someone’s body.

The current working offer is approximately **$100 per month**, but it is not finalized. Do not create direct offer posts, price graphics or firm sales promises until Edison explicitly confirms the offer.

The intended service may include:

The planned sales route is the website. Until the offer is finalized, use educational, mission, personal-story and authority-building content rather than hard sales posts.

## 5. Content objectives

Edison’s two biggest concerns are **reach and sales**. Content should therefore accomplish at least one of these jobs:

1. **Teach:** Give useful fitness, nutrition or execution advice.
2. **Challenge:** Confront excuses, procrastination and weak patterns.
3. **Prove:** Show Edison’s personal process, meals, routines or transformation.
4. **Activate:** Give a workout, checklist, challenge or specific action.

Prioritize saves, shares, profile visits and credible conversations. Do not rely on deceptive engagement bait. A direct question, self-assessment or save prompt is acceptable when it fits the post.

## 6. Content pillars

Use these recurring series labels:

- **EDISON TRAINS:** Workouts, technique, exercise selection and progression
- **EDISON EATS:** Protein, carbohydrates, fiber, calories, meal preparation and food comparisons
- **THE STANDARD:** Discipline, standards, procrastination and execution
- **MY PROOF:** Edison’s transformation, meals, routines and lessons
- **EXECUTION SYSTEMS:** Planning, tracking, check-ins, habits and accountability

Recommended content mix:

- 35% educational nutrition and training
- 25% discipline and accountability
- 20% personal proof and behind-the-scenes process
- 15% actionable workouts and challenges
- 5% offer-related content after the offer is finalized

## 7. Voice and copy

The voice is direct, disciplined, demanding and practical. It should feel like a serious coach who expects action and explains what to do.

Good examples:

- “Your goals record what you repeatedly execute.”
- “Motivation starts. Discipline finishes.”
- “Stop negotiating with the work you already said you would do.”
- “One imperfect meal is not the problem. The 90-day slide is.”

Avoid:

- Generic motivational filler
- Fake aggression without useful instruction
- Excessive profanity
- Guaranteed outcomes
- Invented facts or statistics
- Medical or nutritional certainty beyond the evidence
- Copy that sounds like a corporate brand or an AI assistant

Default copy structure:

1. Hook
2. Truth
3. Mechanism
4. Method
5. Standard
6. Action

Captions should use short paragraphs, concrete language and readable spacing. Most captions should end with one useful prompt rather than several competing calls to action.

## 9. Standard formats

### Cinematic carousel

- Six to nine slides
- One image and one central idea per slide
- Strong hook on slide one
- Brief supporting copy
- Numbered slides
- Overview image, caption and posting ZIP

### Educational infographic

- Cream editorial or obsidian technical background
- Large, accurate food or exercise imagery
- Values in gold
- Crimson reserved for contrast or warnings
- State units and preparation assumptions

### Typography statement

- Main line should fill the useful canvas
- Maximum approximately 12 words on the primary slide
- One gold emphasis phrase
- Minimal decorative elements

### Reel

- Hook in the first second
- Visual change every 0.5–1.5 seconds
- Keep essential text between y=250 and y=1580
- Keep the lower interface area free of critical information
- Provide both a version with original sound and a silent version when useful

## 10. Repository workflow

Before changing files:

1. Inspect `brand/CONTENT_SYSTEM.md`.
2. Inspect the closest existing output for structural precedent.
3. Preserve all unrelated files and user changes.

Store deliverables under:

`outputs/edison-ambrister-<content-name>/`

Typical package:

- Numbered final PNG files or final MP4
- `preview.png` or `preview.jpg`
- `overview.jpg` for carousels
- `caption.txt`
- `README.md`
- `alt-text.txt` when appropriate
- Source imagery
- Editable SVGs or a reproducible generator
- `final-carousel.zip` for carousel posts

Reusable generators belong in `tools/`.

Use the canonical Edison Ambrister logo from `brand/assets/edison-ambrister/`, not a copy found in an older output folder.

After building:

1. Verify every image’s dimensions.
2. Inspect the cover and information-heavy slides visually.
3. Confirm that no text covers the subject.
4. Confirm exact footer wording.
5. Confirm nutrition units, workout prescription and spelling.
6. Test ZIP files.
7. Run `git diff --check`.
8. Commit with a clear description.
9. Push the authorized finished work to `main`.
10. Verify the local and remote commit hashes match.

Do not publish partial, unreviewed renders.

## 12. Known lessons from previous revisions

- Edison prefers 4:5 posts rather than square carousels.
- Headlines should be large and use the available canvas.
- Small, timid typography feels off-brand.
- Image subjects must remain unobstructed.
- The Edison Ambrister horizontal logo belongs on every designed post when space allows; use the EA monogram only when the full name would be illegible.
- The footer must say `DISCIPLINE - ACCOUNTABILITY - CONSISTENCY`.
- Gold is the principal accent; crimson is secondary.
- Edison prefers polished, cinematic and premium work over generic templates.
- He will reject work that looks obviously AI-generated, structurally weak or visually cheap.
- Download links should point to GitHub raw files because chat attachments have previously been difficult for him to download.

## 13. How to collaborate with Edison

Edison wants help executing, not only brainstorming. When he gives a clear topic:

1. Improve the concept.
2. Write the final copy.
3. Create the asset.
4. Review it at phone size.
5. Correct visible problems.
6. Package and publish it.
7. Give direct GitHub viewing and download links.

Do not repeatedly ask for confirmation on routine design decisions. Ask only when a missing fact would materially change the content or make a claim inaccurate.

When Edison dislikes something, identify the visual reason, correct it and show the revised result. Do not defend a weak design.
