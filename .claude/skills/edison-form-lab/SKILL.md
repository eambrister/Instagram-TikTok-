# Edison Form Lab

Read `../_shared/edison-video-standard.md` first. Series identity: **EDISON TRAINS — FORM LAB**.

## Non-negotiables
- **Voice is isolated** with a real speech-separation stage (Resolve Voice Isolation or a neural enhancer) before mixing. EQ, compression or ducking alone is not isolation; never blend the noisy original back in.
- **The grade is visible:** deep blacks with detail, controlled bright signs, restrained environment colour, slightly warm skin. Compare source vs grade before rendering.
- **Clean type, no caption boxes:** ivory Barlow Condensed captions, single crimson emphasis word, soft shadow only.
- **Teach through the footage:** keep the movement, positions and useful reps readable; keep ≥96% of the coaching (100% preferred), qualifications and ending.
- **Graphics follow real movement:** fresh landmark tracking and same-frame masks. Show the relevant muscles and mechanics; when clothing hides anatomy, use a clearly labelled illustration, not a fake heat map. Never invent activation %, loads, angles or health claims.
- **Opening ident:** CTD logo animated over moving footage and coaching — no long silent logo leader.

## Workflow
1. Ingest, verify fps/orientation/audio/transfer; transcribe; map setup → movement → working muscle → control → takeaway. Create a fresh project folder per episode (`outputs/form-lab-<exercise>/`).
2. Prove sound and colour first (level-matched before/after voice, source/grade stills).
3. Design composition from this episode's own cues and muscles — never recycle another episode's labels.
4. Build: grade footage before compositing artwork; duck music under speech, lift through demos; accents on meaningful events.
5. Verify per the shared standard; include a matched voice comparison when listening isn't available.
6. **Cover:** oversized ivory/gold exercise name, CTD logo, dark gym frame, muscle-group subtitle. **Caption:** concrete hook, actionable form cues, useful close, five hashtags.

Reuse identity, hierarchy, process and quality gates across the series; rebuild transcript, tracking, annotations and timing every episode. Planning a series is not a request to create empty jobs or publish.
