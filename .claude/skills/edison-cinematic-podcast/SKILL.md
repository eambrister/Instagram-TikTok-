# Edison Cinematic Podcast

Read `../_shared/edison-video-standard.md` and `brand/monumental/STYLE_GUIDE.md` first.

## Decisions
- **People first.** Cut at thought changes, handoffs, qualifications and payoffs. With two cameras: close → stacked two-shot → close when both people add meaning. With one camera: honest reframes and relevant inserts; never invent a reaction or angle.
- **Palette:** obsidian ink, bone `#EEE9DF` copy, muted gold emphasis. Bodoni Moda for titles and rare thesis cards; Barlow Condensed / Montserrat Bold for fast captions; Montserrat Medium for utility text.
- **Each layer has one job:** word captions carry pace; topic cards organize lists; notification graphics show a real event or a clearly marked illustration; serif/sculpture inserts carry a major thesis. Don't stack them on a face.
- **Classical sculpture** is a separate, generated, text-free environment or prop — monumental but secondary. Never turn Edison or a guest into a statue, and never generate a new speaking body.
- **Meaning:** keep negations, uncertainty and qualifications; fix transcript spellings from footage or verified material.

## Workflow
Inspect footage, duration, aspect, fps, audio → source-relative transcript; inventory cameras and real evidence → plan story and clean dialogue first → assign layout beats, caption groups, cards, graphics and inserts in a cue sheet → render in the available editor (Resolve on Edison's machine, or `video/` HyperFrames here for caption-led clips) → preview at speed and verify per the shared standard.

**Landscape reels with advanced 3D titles:** use real projected geometry or Fusion 3D for anything called 3D; animated stills are 2.5D and must be described that way.
