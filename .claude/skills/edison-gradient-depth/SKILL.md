# Edison Gradient Depth

Read `../_shared/edison-video-standard.md` first. Two modes:
- **Talking + workout hybrid** (default when footage mixes both): first-person copy in Edison's voice, gradient/glow/outline depth captions over the real lines, talking motion choreography, silhouettes, mask repair; speech meaning protected.
- **Montage:** music-led, ~60 s, 1080×1920, 30 fps.

## Select before decorating
1. Inventory footage (name, origin, duration, dimensions, fps, audio). Keep camera-profile evidence separate from assumptions.
2. Sample frames across candidates; reject other heroes, empty equipment, long setups, hidden faces, waiting. Find effort, the decisive phase of a rep, a direct glance, a grip, an approach to camera, a human payoff.
3. Review chosen ranges densely — confirm the lift actually happens there.
4. Deduplicate by filename and hash; use each source once (one contiguous excerpt incl. its pre-roll).
5. Write a specific premise: opening action → change into work → payoff.

## Rhythm and composition
Mostly 0.8–1.5 s inserts with 2.3–3.7 s hero passages; vary size, exercise, viewpoint, environment. Close-match layout: a 1080×1350 active image at y=285 on a 1080×1920 obsidian→graphite gradient canvas (so the post crop also works). Short condensed titles with hierarchy, in Edison's voice — e.g. **EXECUTE.**, **THE STANDARD.**, **EARN IT.**, **NO NEGOTIATION.** — write fresh copy per premise.

## Signature effects (a few, sustained)
- Gradient silhouette (graphite → crimson → gold, Edison's palette in place of blue-amber) resolving into the real moving person.
- Contours that expand, converge and follow the body for a legible interval.
- Large titles between background and the exact-frame foreground subject.
- Incoming athlete before incoming room, with true continuous pre-roll.
- Selective diagonal reveals, brief lens smears, eased pushes, rules and arcs on major beats.
Never reset a resolved silhouette at the handoff; never fake a moving cutout with a still.

## Finish
Balance skin shot by shot; cool the environment selectively; keep black clothing detail. Music with anticipation → change → finish, authorized and logged. Layered air sweeps, impacts, metallic accents, clicks; synthesized sound labelled. Verify per the shared standard; never label a new cut approved before Edison's feedback.
