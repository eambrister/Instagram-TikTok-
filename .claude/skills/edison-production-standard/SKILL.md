## Shooting guidance (B-roll that edits well)
- **Build the life, film the evidence:** meal prep and repeatable meals, the food scale and tracking app, early training, walking to the gym, hands on equipment, check-in moments. Real process beats staged luxury.
- For each action get a wide, a medium, a tight detail and a low angle; hold every shot 5–8 s; lock off a tripod shot for clone/depth effects (plus a few seconds of empty clean plate).
- Phone/pocket gimbal: shoot 4K at 24/30 fps (60 for slow motion), fixed white balance, lock exposure on skin, and note whether a log profile was used.
- Long-form episodes: record camera and screen separately; plan a 12–16 minute structure with chapters.
