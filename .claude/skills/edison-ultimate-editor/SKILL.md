# Edison Ultimate Editor

Read `../_shared/edison-video-standard.md` first. This master unifies the other `edison-*` editing skills; use `edison-production-standard` to pick techniques. Don't impose every style on one film. A new combined result needs Edison's own approval.

## Establish the film
Current instructions set footage, participants, music, aspect, length and delivery. Only the requested folder is camera media. Inspect every recording (representative frames + transcript with original-relative times); watch selected action densely. Find three openings internally, execute the strongest. A vlog develops through real conversation, effort, teaching and human moments — don't silently shrink it into a montage.

## Go harder by default
Native Fusion over baked overlays where practical, real per-shot node grades, punch-ins, speed ramps, designed sound and more mask moments — within the honesty rules.

## Minimal premium mode (YouTube talking-head / vlog)
Two-font captions (Barlow Condensed + Montserrat), a single accent colour (gold `#D6A918`), paper textures, line-led scenes, defocused A-roll under graphics, and the rule **announce → face, explain → visual**. This mode overrides heavier go-harder effects for those edits. Short vertical vlog Reels → `edison-vlog-reel60`.

## Build in Resolve
Functional bins; separate picture, dialogue, music, SFX, graphics and foreground tracks. Ledger of source in/out, output frames, crop, speed, masks, graphics, audio provenance. Versioned checkpoints and DRP; new named timelines for material revisions.

- **Grade inside Resolve:** confirm the profile, normalize only when established, balance per shot, then graphite/obsidian environments, controlled highlights, natural skin; real product colours. One creative finish. Don't bake an external grade and call it a Resolve grade.
- **Isolate the voice:** Resolve Voice Isolation or neural separation, then conservative EQ, dynamics, levelling. Keep identity, speed and sync. ≥96% retention only when a full-message treatment is requested.
- **Annotations from content:** phrase captions, occasional big thesis words, exercise guides — separate jobs, nothing over faces. Form cues follow the visible exercise; labelled schematics where clothing hides anatomy; never invent loads, angles, percentages, records or health claims.
- **Spatial graphics:** fresh mattes for depth titles, silhouettes, contours, handoffs, echoes; matched transforms; real Fusion 3D (scene/camera/light/renderer) or projected mesh for anything called 3D, with a teaching or story purpose. Grade footage before compositing.
- **Soundtrack:** authorized music only, offsets logged, under speech, lifted through demos; contrast via anticipation, selective impact, decay — no constant whoosh layer.

## Prove and deliver
Proof grade, isolated dialogue, 3D/type and hard masks before full export; a populated node graph is not proof. Deliver MP4, DRP, ledger, transcript/captions, assets, grade settings, sound provenance; baked vs native clearly separated; verify per the shared standard. Covers/social copy only when requested; gym covers → `edison-gym-reel-cover`.
