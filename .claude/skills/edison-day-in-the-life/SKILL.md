# Edison Day In The Life

Read `../_shared/edison-video-standard.md` first. Fits MY PROOF and EXECUTION SYSTEMS.

## Creative contract
- Progress through real actions: wake/routine → focused work → meal prep (EDISON EATS: repeatable meals, protein) → preparation → training → a real payoff. Keep meaningful quiet beats; cut idle setup. Energy from contrast and escalation, not constant effects.
- Fresh source intervals; match gestures; incoming masked subject and its scene share continuous source time. No recycled actions to pad length.
- Titles: heavy Barlow Condensed display, controlled tracking, dimensional edges, restrained brushed-gold/silver lighting, staggered entry, and an animated rotating slash in crimson when requested. Readable at phone size even when his body passes in front. No bounce/pop captions, no permanent logo (CTD on the end card only).
- Grade: obsidian depth, controlled highlights, blue-neutral graphite shadows, warm readable skin. Match rooms before the look; no assumed DJI log, blanket blue tint or crushed skin.
- Masks per frame for textured clones, ivory silhouettes and time echoes; hold out chairs, mirrors, equipment and other people. Prove a mask fix on each shot rather than globally.
- Effects motivated by action and music: refractive angled wipes, camera whips, short perspective-panel reveals. Real geometry when calling it 3D; final camera motion on picture + mask + graphics together.
- Sound: controlled opening, selective sweeps/risers, short music dips before big arrivals, a few substantial impacts.

## Production
Frame-addressed timeline with source paths, in/out, grade/crop and transition pre-roll. Prove titles, masks, skin and transitions before full render. If a phone or screen clip is unreadable, don't invent its contents. Keep real chronology (a night clip is labelled as night). Deliver per the shared standard, plus Reels cover (real frame, crop-safe) and caption when requested.
