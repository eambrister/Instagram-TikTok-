# Edison Full-Message Talking Head

Read `../_shared/edison-video-standard.md` first.

## Contract
- **Cut the raw footage first** (shared standard §4b): remove dead air, flubbed retakes, stutters, filler and restated lines. Then keep ≥96% of the *message*: every point, example, number, qualification and the ending. This is measured on meaning, not raw word count. Keep order, speed, logic, examples, qualifications and the ending. A later explicit request for a short extract overrides this.
- Engagement comes from framing, layered type, graphics, sound and transitions — not from cutting the message.
- Deliver a playable edit; a storyboard is not completion.

## Workflow
1. **Understand the take.** Full transcript with word times; mark arguments and emotional turns. Check uncertain words against the audio; never turn a guess into a caption.
2. **Lock the message.** Record every removal and why. Run a retention audit (word-time comparison of source vs edit) and a manual meaning check.
3. **Motion map.** Timecoded list: spoken trigger → visual purpose → caption zone → sound cue. Patterns: depth title behind the body, typing/loading reveal for a list or process, number build for a real figure Edison states, split cards for before/after, thesis word in Bodoni Moda gold. Keep developing to the end.
4. **Prove depth text** on short samples first: per-frame subject masks, text behind the real body, check hair, shoulders and hands. A static silhouette or rectangle is not acceptable.
5. **Build and mix.** Skin-aware grade; voice cleaned and dominant; captions and cards designed together so they never fight for space.
6. **Verify** per the shared standard and report the retention percentage.

## Harder mode
When Edison asks for "harder" / "more intense": add a visual escalation arc (clean → layered → signature passage → calm close), frame-aware staging around his position, pacing from real gestures, and higher-contrast sound — message retention unchanged.
