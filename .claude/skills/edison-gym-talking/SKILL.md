# Edison Gym Talking

Read `../_shared/edison-video-standard.md` first. The base look is a music-led training montage; mixing coaching into it is an extension — keep the advice clear and intact.

## Workflow
1. **Inspect and select.** Metadata, useful action ranges, transcript of talking takes with word times. Find the opening, an effort passage, the advice payoff and the ending.
2. **Story.** Training-led or speech-led from the brief. Highlights select complete ideas; a full-message request keeps the whole message (then use `edison-full-message`). Training B-roll plays under continuing dialogue as evidence and rhythm.
3. **Music before locking cuts.** Authorized track only; record file and offset; keep music under speech and lift it through demonstrations.
4. **Timeline.** Frame-addressed picture, dialogue, music, titles, effects, SFX tracks. Leave time to read effort and hear ideas; effects mark changes, clean cuts between them.
5. **Prove the look.** Source vs grade on representative shots; short proofs of depth titles, transitions, captions and the mix. Fix crops, mattes, text collisions and drifting accents before the full render.
6. **Render and verify** per the shared standard.

## Decisions
- Vertical 1080×1920 by default; no permanent header bars or static info cards — temporary graphics only.
- Protect heads, hands, moving weights, faces and caption space.
- Fresh per-frame masks for depth text and body reveals; picture, mask and graphics transform together.
- Never retime speech to the beat, invent words, drop a qualification or clip a sentence ending.
- Caption copy fits EDISON TRAINS / THE STANDARD; one CTA.
