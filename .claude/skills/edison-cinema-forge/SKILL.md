# Edison Cinema Forge

Read `../_shared/edison-video-standard.md` first. Combines the action progression of `edison-day-in-the-life`, the moving-body depth of `edison-gradient-depth`, the message integrity of `edison-full-message` and the human rhythm of `edison-cinematic-podcast`. Take creative responsibility — deliver a film, not a brief.

Defaults: 1080×1920, 24 or 30 fps, 40–60 s montage when no length is given; dialogue-led films run as long as the message needs.

## Select and direct
- When asked to review all footage: inventory every file, log unreadable ones, deduplicate copies, sample frames across every file, then watch chosen intervals densely. Sampling is not a full watch.
- Hero shots centre Edison. Alternate wide context, medium action, tight tactile detail, low angles, and real reactions only. Keep real event order; a multi-day montage must not pretend to be one day.
- Actions before effects. One contiguous appearance per recording unless a callback adds meaning. Keep hands, equipment and interaction in frame; use designed windows/splits for meaningful landscape footage.

## Image and type
Match exposure and white balance first, then the obsidian/graphite look with warm skin and restrained gold metal accents. Titles: real font metrics, deliberate tracking, bevel/extrusion, moving light, restrained stagger, inside phone-safe margins. Depth titles only with per-frame masks. Copy is editorial — never invent money results, quotes or facts from unreadable screens.

## Motion and effects
Escalate from clean cuts and eased moves to a few signature passages: action-matched whips, refractive apertures, projected panels, silhouette/texture echoes, 3D objects with a purpose. "3D" means real perspective geometry, lighting and camera; extruded raster type is 2.5D. Masked incoming foreground and its scene share continuous source time; final camera motion moves picture + mask + graphics together. Repair failed masks; never substitute a frozen cutout.

## Interviews and sound
Transcribe with source times; keep meaning and emotional progression (≥96% for a full-message request; coherent excerpts for highlights). J/L cuts, honest reframes, relevant B-roll, dialogue cleanup, restrained captions, occasional thesis card. Authorized music with recorded offset; a few substantial impacts, sweeps and tactile cues; meter the encoded mix.

## Package
Caption (Monumental caption system, one CTA, five hashtags, keyword bank) and a cover from a real hero frame: obsidian/gold, dimensional title, brief ivory-silhouette accent, CTD logo. Deliver both with a new film unless excluded.
