# Edison Promo

Read `../_shared/edison-video-standard.md` first. **Offer gate:** the coaching offer is not final — do not make price, "DM me" or sales promos until Edison confirms it. Mission, announcement, story and education promos are fine.

## Signature
1. **Voice first:** real neural speech enhancement; keep lip sync, consonants and natural texture. A light FFmpeg denoise is not enough when gym noise remains.
2. **Grade:** warm readable skin, defined arms, controlled cool surroundings, anchored blacks with detail. Confirmed profile transform only.
3. **Gold editorial captions:** complete short sentence-case phrases in Bodoni Moda Bold, gold `#D6A918` (pale gold `#F3D878` on dark shirts), quiet fade in/out. No karaoke word pops, bounce, thick outline, caption box or orange. Large titles in Bodoni Moda Black; small labels in widely tracked Montserrat.
4. **Opaque moving silhouette:** an exact per-frame body mask turns ivory briefly, then reveals the graded person feet-to-head, marked by a shutter click. Works mid-shot; not a translucent gradient wash.
5. **Message graphics:** animate what is actually said — a milestone, a nutrition diagram, a countdown, the website `cutthroatdiscipline.netlify.app` lockup. Protect face and hands.
6. **Dialogue-led sound:** opening riser, clicks on silhouette events, music ducked under speech. Authorized music and SFX only.

## Workflow
Probe, transcribe, inspect frames; identify names, places and the real CTA before authoring graphics → frame-addressed timeline (phrases, graphics, silhouette events, cues) → isolate dialogue before music → grade before graphics → per-frame central-person masks, background people excluded → a few meaningful sequences, one dominant idea at a time → render, verify, deliver full mix, optional dialogue/FX-only version, SRT, ledger and provenance. Creative track choice ≠ music clearance.
