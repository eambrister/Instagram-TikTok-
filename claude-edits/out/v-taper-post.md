## Caption (Instagram, TikTok, YouTube description)

Gents, if you want a V-taper physique, there are a few things you have to focus on.

1. Back. Lat pull-downs and pull-ups. Pull-ups are king for the width of your back.
2. Side delts. The front and rear delts make your shoulders pop, but the middle delt pushes the sides out and makes your upper body wider.
3. A small waist. You can't get a V-taper with a big belly. Ideally your chest is at least 10 inches wider than your waist.

For me: a 38-inch chest and a 26-inch waist.
