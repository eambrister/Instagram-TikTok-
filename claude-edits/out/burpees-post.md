## Caption (Instagram, TikTok, YouTube description)

Today I completed 10,000 burpees. 100 burpees every day for 100 days. Here's what I learned.

1. Sometimes you just have to do things you don't want to do. Some days I was so tired, but I still did it, and I reaped the benefits.
2. Consistency beats intensity. 100 burpees a day beat trying to do 1,000 in one workout. The habits stack up.
3. Stop thinking about the whole journey. I didn't focus on the 10,000. I focused on the 100 I had to do that day.

Focus on the small, incremental steps to get to your goal.
